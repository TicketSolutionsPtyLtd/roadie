# @oztix/roadie-editor

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 9ee8bb9: Add a block handle to the `blocks` preset, to move, copy, and delete blocks by drag, menu, or keyboard.

  A grip shows beside the hovered block, or the caret's while the editor has focus. Dragging it moves the block as one undo step, keeping its `blockId`. Its menu, also opened with `Mod+/`, has Move up, Move down, Duplicate, and Delete, so keyboard, screen reader, and touch users can do everything a drag does. A framed `blocks` editor makes room for the grip at the start of its text.

- a1f2c77: Add Turn into, Insert below, and Copy link to block to the block menu. Turn into makes a text block a paragraph, a heading, a list, or a quote, keeping its `blockId`; Insert below opens a line under the block with the insert menu; and Copy link to block copies the page's address with the block's `blockId` as its hash, which scrolls the editor to the block when opened, and says Link copied by the grip. Move up and Move down now leave the menu open on the moved block. The menu opens beside the grip, or below it where there's no room, and `Mod+/` opens it with its first action ready. On a phone the grip has a 44px target, and the text is inset at its start by the grip's room, in a `subtler` editor too, with no add button beside it. The menu opens as a half-height sheet below its block, led by Move up, Move down and an accent Done, with no scrim, as Settings has, with Turn into and Move to as steps of their own and Delete last. Move to puts the block under one of the page's headings or sections. A `blocks` editor makes room for its grip at its start on a desktop too, in a `subtler` editor as well, so neither its host nor a frame clips it. The grip lines up with a section's first line of text rather than its padding, follows the editor as it resizes, and after a tap follows the caret onto a new line. A section that starts with an image or another block puts the grip at that block's top.
- 31193c7: Add block selection to editors with a block handle. `Escape` selects the caret's block, and `Shift+ArrowUp` and `Shift+ArrowDown` take in the blocks above or below. With several blocks selected, by these keys or by a text selection across them, Move up, Move down, Duplicate, Delete, Turn into, and their keys act on them all, and the block menu is headed with how many, such as "3 blocks". Selected blocks take one accent ring, rounded as a section's, the same as a selected image or custom block (which had none before), and the floating toolbar hides. One selected block keeps its own menu, headed by its type. Undo after deleting selected blocks brings them back selected. Typing or `Enter` carries on at the end of the selection, rather than replacing it. A second `Escape` goes on to whatever is around the editor, such as a dialog. Move up and Move down keep the menu on a section as it moves. Typing over a selection that runs out of a section no longer comes out reversed in a new section. A block dragged by its handle now lands before the block under the pointer, or after it once the pointer is past its middle.
- 28a24f9: Add `settings` to an `extensions` block, so its view can show it as the page will rather than as a form. Settings leads the block's menu, and Enter on the selected block opens it: a drawer from the side, or from the bottom on a phone, with a field for each `text` or `choice` setting. Each change shows as it's made, and Done, Escape, Enter in a text setting or a click away keeps the changes as one undo step. A `choice` setting's `defaultValue` shows while its attr is unset. `EditorSetting` and `EditorSettingOption` are exported from `@oztix/roadie-editor/extensions`.

  Settings also take `image` (`{ src, alt }`, with an upload through `onUploadImage`), `action` (`{ label, href }`), and `list` types: a list's items are added, removed, reordered with `Sortable`, and edited in their own folds. With a mouse, a click on a block with settings opens them at the text clicked, a list item included. The drawer has Done and says that Undo puts changes back. Closing puts the caret back where it was before the block was selected, or after the block. On a wide page it makes room for the block beside it.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 0b24b01: First release of `@oztix/roadie-editor`, the rich-text `Editor` on Tiptap v3.

  A rich-text field that saves its content as an `EditorValue`, `{ version, preset, doc }`, in JSON, never HTML. Three subpaths ship:

  - `@oztix/roadie-editor/editor`: `Editor`, a rich-text field. `preset='basic'` (the default) holds paragraphs, lists, bold, italic, underline, and links. `preset='rich'` adds headings 2 to 4, quotes, dividers, images (through `onUploadImage`), embeds from YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify, and TikTok, and tables. It takes `value`, `defaultValue`, and `onValueChange`, plus `placeholder`, `readOnly`, `disabled`, `size` (the `Prose` sizes), and `emphasis` (`normal`, `subtle`, or `subtler` for content edited in place). In a `Field` it takes its label, description, `invalid`, `required`, and `disabled`, and `name` submits the value with a native form. Its code loads after the page, behind a skeleton; `preloadEditor()` starts it sooner, and `useEditorContext()` gives custom toolbar controls the Tiptap editor. Import `@oztix/roadie-editor/css` after the components' CSS.
  - `@oztix/roadie-editor/schema`: the saved format, with no React, so it runs on a server. `presets`, `migrate` (brings a stored value up to `CURRENT_VERSION`, or into another preset, dropping what the preset can't hold), `createEmptyValue`, and the embed helpers `parseEmbedUrl`, `embedSrc`, `embedShareUrl`, and `defaultEmbedProviders`.
  - `@oztix/roadie-editor/render`: `renderEditorValue` returns React elements in `Prose`, and `renderEditorHtml` an HTML string in `.prose`, with none of the editor's code.

  Unsafe links, such as `javascript:`, never reach a saved value: the editor refuses them, pasted HTML loses them, and `migrate` and the renderers drop them and keep their text. An embed saves its provider and id, never a URL.
