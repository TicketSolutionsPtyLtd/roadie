The pattern gives Oztix work texture and energy, and a look that's
recognisably Oztix without overpowering the content.

## Partnership flow

The partnership flow is the brand's pattern. Its curves interlock to show
movement and connection, the partnerships the brand is built on. The same
swirl carries across every piece while its crop and colour adapt.

## Colour

- Blue is the foundation, and works in every market.
- Music and venue work brings in purple.
- Shows, events, and sports bring in orange.

The pattern keeps its light colours in dark mode, like all brand artwork.

## Layout

- Crop the pattern to a rectangle to frame a page, as a border or a
  background.
- Layer it behind and in front of a photo's subject to add depth.
- Pair it with a black strip that holds the logo and tagline, for branded
  headers and footers.
- Set it behind content to add texture without competing for attention.
- Crop and scale it freely, as long as the curves stay whole.

## Guidelines

- Use the pattern as a key visual across marketing.
- Let it bleed off the edges of the page.
- Keep its proportions when you scale it.
- Leave enough contrast for any text set over it, and keep critical text off
  its busiest parts.
- Don't stretch, distort, rotate, or angle the pattern.
- Don't change its shapes or line weights.
- Don't use colours outside the brand palette.
- Don't scale it so small that the flow is lost.
- Don't let it overpower the key content.
