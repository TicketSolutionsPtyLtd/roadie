The logo is the most recognisable part of the brand. Using it the same way
every time keeps Oztix work professional and easy to recognise in every market.

## Elements

The logo has three parts.

- The logomark, a swirl for a dynamic and innovative approach.
- The wordmark, "oztix".
- The tagline, Built on Partnerships, the brand's position. It keeps its title
  case.

## Colour

The logo comes in three colours, so it stays legible on any background.

- Brand, in [Spotlight blue](/brand/colour), goes on white or light
  backgrounds. It's the first choice.
- Dark is for one colour print, light backgrounds where blue lacks contrast,
  and sitting beside other neutral logos.
- Light goes on dark backgrounds, photos, and colour fills.

## Choosing a logo

1. Start with the primary logo and tagline, in blue, on white or a
   transparent background.
2. Make sure the logo contrasts clearly with what's behind it.
3. Keep the tagline wherever there's room.
4. Once a piece or campaign has a logo, use the same one across all of it.

## Clear space

Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the x in "oztix".
Keep text, images, and edges out of it.

## Minimum size

The minimums are the same with or without the tagline.

| Logo      | Screen          | Print         |
| --------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| Primary   | 24px tall       | 1cm tall      |
| Secondary | 48px wide       | 2cm wide      |

Product interfaces can set the primary logo at 20px tall. Marketing and
partner work keeps the 24px minimum.

## Sharing the logo

Share the logo with partners for co-branded promotion and to recognise a
partnership. Always send the partner logo pack, never a single file, an old
version, or an edited logo.

## Guidelines

- Don't change the logo's colours.
- Don't add effects, such as shadows or glows.
- Don't stretch, distort, or rotate the logo.
- Don't rearrange the logo's parts.
- Don't place the logo on a busy background without enough contrast.
- Don't use the wordmark without the logomark.
- Don't use the logomark alone, except as a favicon or app icon.

## In Roadie

The [Logo](/components/logo) component draws the primary logo in apps, in
Spotlight blue. Its `size` sets the height from 20px to 48px, `variant='mark'`
shows the logomark alone, and `product` names an Oztix product beside the
mark. The `wordmark` variant is for a logo collapsing to or from the mark, not
a logo of its own.

```tsx-live layout=stack gap=4
<Logo size='xl' />
<Logo product='Studio' size='lg' />
<Logo variant='mark' size='lg' />
```
