Illustrations tell the brand's story and give people something to connect
with. They celebrate the energy of live events with bold, dynamic
compositions, strong brand colour, and clean, modern line work, always
focused on people and the connections between them.

## Types

Illustrations come in three tiers, from the most detailed to the simplest.

### Hero illustrations

Hero illustrations are the most detailed, for high impact marketing. They're
complex, narrative scenes with three or more people in an event setting, rich
with detail and atmosphere. Use them for hero sections, major campaigns,
feature launches, and key landing pages, at 1200px wide or more. The hero set
is still being drawn.

### Feature illustrations

Feature illustrations make a complex idea easy to grasp. Each tells one story
about a single feature or service, often through a metaphor, with one or two
people or a purely conceptual scene. In product, keep them literal and
focused on what the feature does. In marketing, lean into the benefit and the
excitement. Use them for feature pages, sales material, secondary marketing,
and email headers, at 600px to 1200px wide.

### Spot illustrations

Spot illustrations are simple, single ideas for interfaces and supporting
content. They have few elements, usually no people, and a direct visual
metaphor. Use them for empty states, section dividers, help content, and
small marketing pieces, at 48px to 300px wide.

The illustration pack's key illustrations are dimensional spots, drawn in
house purple and orange with a Spotlight blue side.

## Choosing a type

| When it's for                          | Use                    |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| A major campaign or landing page       | A hero illustration    |
| Explaining a feature or service        | A feature illustration |
| An interface element or small piece    | A spot illustration    |
| A service category or brand message    | A brand pillar spot    |

## Markets

Change what an illustration shows for each market, never its style.

| Market     | Show                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Live music | Concert scenes, performances, and audience energy       |
| Venues     | Architectural spaces, crowd flow, and facilities        |
| Shows      | Exhibition spaces, interactive displays, and attendees  |
| Sports     | Movement, competition, and spectators                   |
| Events     | Community, shared experiences, and atmosphere           |

## Usage

- Match an illustration's detail to the space and screen size it fills.
- Save hero illustrations for the biggest moments.
- Check load time and scaling on every device.
- For print, use 300dpi or more, convert colour for CMYK, and leave
  generous margins.

## Making illustrations

- Build from the brand's base shapes.
- Give each illustration a clear focal point.
- Keep perspective and scale consistent across a series.
- Use the brand palette with purpose, and build depth with colour.
- Keep enough contrast for the illustration to read.

## Guidelines

- Choose the type that fits the context.
- Keep each type's style consistent.
- Make sure the illustration supports the message.
- Don't mix illustration styles in one project.
- Don't use a detailed illustration in a small space.
- Don't use colours outside the brand palette.
