Illustrations tell the brand's story with bold compositions, strong brand
colour, and clean line work, always focused on people and the connections
between them.

## Types

Illustrations come in three tiers, from the most detailed to the simplest.

<PairTable>

| Tier    | Use                                                                                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Hero    | Narrative scenes with three or more people, for campaigns and landing pages, 1200px wide or more. The hero set is still being drawn. |
| Feature | One story about a feature or service, often a metaphor, for feature pages, sales material, and email headers, 600px to 1200px wide.  |
| Spot    | A single idea with few elements, for empty states, dividers, help content, and small pieces, 48px to 300px wide.                     |

</PairTable>

In product, keep feature illustrations literal and focused on what the
feature does. In marketing, lean into the benefit and the excitement.

## Making illustrations

- Build from the brand's base shapes, with a clear focal point.
- Keep perspective, scale, and style consistent across a series.
- Use the brand palette with purpose, and keep enough contrast to read.
- Match the detail to the space. Don't use a detailed illustration small.
- For print, use 300dpi or more and convert colour for CMYK.
