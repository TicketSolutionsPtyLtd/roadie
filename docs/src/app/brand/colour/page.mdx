The brand palette is Roadie's colour scales, so a design and the app it becomes
use the same values. Each colour has a lighting name that says what it's for,
like the lights on a stage.

## Lighting names

Each lighting name is a step on a Roadie colour scale. Use the name in briefs
and design files, and the step in code.

| Name      | Roadie step   | What it lights                                  |
| --------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Flood     | Neutral 0     | Pure white backgrounds, and text on dark ones   |
| Wash      | 2             | Subtle backgrounds, rarely used                 |
| House     | 5             | Patterns, text blocks, and container backgrounds |
| Stage     | 7             | Secondary actions, links, and borders           |
| Spotlight | 9             | Primary calls to action and hero moments        |
| Backstage | 11            | Secondary text                                  |
| Blackout  | 12            | Body text, headings, and dark backgrounds       |

The swatches show each colour's light value. Brand artwork, such as print,
pattern, and illustration, keeps these values in dark mode too.

## Primary colours

### Neutral

Neutral carries most text and backgrounds. Blackout is for body text and
headings, and Backstage for secondary text.

## Tertiary colours

Green, yellow, and red are status colours. Use them sparingly, for what they
mean.

### Green

Green is for success and growth, such as positive outcomes and metrics.

<BrandSwatches hue='green' />

### Yellow

Yellow draws attention to featured content. Use it very sparingly. Spotlight
yellow is darker than a pure yellow so it holds contrast, and yellow fills
always take dark text.

<BrandSwatches hue='yellow' />

### Red

Red is for critical and urgent states only, such as errors and sold out
events.

<BrandSwatches hue='red' />

The neutral steps other than Flood, the yellow steps, and the Backstage steps
now follow Roadie's values, and their print values are still to come.

## Usage

### Digital and social

- Lead with Spotlight blue, so the work is recognisably Oztix.
- Use house colours for patterns and text blocks.
- Pick one spotlight colour per design as the hero, and never use more than
  two in one piece.
- Keep bright colours off large backgrounds. The pattern is the exception,
  with house colour curves on a spotlight ground.
- Don't mix warm and cool spotlight colours.
- Keep blue in every Oztix logo and primary call to action.

### Print and outdoor

- Use house colours for large areas. They print more cheaply and evenly.
- Keep spotlight colours off large print backgrounds.
- Specify CMYK values, and don't assume RGB values print the same.
- Test colour combinations on the actual paper stock.

### Markets

Each market pairs blue with a secondary colour.

| Market | Colours                                           |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Music  | Blue and purple. Purple can lead the background.  |
| Sports | Blue and orange, in high energy combinations.     |
| Venues | Blue and purple, mostly blue, kept professional.  |
| Shows  | Blue and orange, matched to the show's energy.    |
| Events | Blue, orange, and green, the most flexible range. |

### Partners and co-branding

- Keep Spotlight blue for every Oztix brand element.
- Make the partnership clear in the hierarchy.
- Use neutrals for shared space and house colours for supporting elements.
- Don't drop Oztix blue, use a partner's colours for Oztix calls to action, or
  mix colours that muddy both brands.

### Common pieces

| Piece         | Colour                                                                 |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email         | Spotlight blue headers, house blue backgrounds, and Blackout text      |
| Social post   | One spotlight colour leads, with house colour support                  |
| Print         | House colours for main areas, and spotlight for brand moments          |
| Presentation  | Spotlight blue, house patterns and text blocks, and neutral backgrounds |

## Contrast

Contrast is measured with APCA, at the minimums on
[Colors](/foundations/colors#contrast): Lc 75 for body text, Lc 60 for labels
on strong fills and large text, and Lc 45 for non-text elements.

White text on Spotlight blue passes for labels and large text. Check every
combination with colour blindness simulation too, and never use colour alone
to carry meaning.

## In Roadie

- The house colours are the `brand-blue`, `brand-purple`, and `brand-orange`
  intents. Emphasis picks the step, so code names the intent rather than a
  lighting name. See [Colors](/foundations/colors).
- Artwork that keeps its light colours in dark mode uses the pinned
  `--color-{scale}-light-{step}` tokens.
- Every step of every scale is on [Color scales](/tokens/color-scales).
