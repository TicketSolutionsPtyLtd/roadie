The brand palette is Roadie's colour scales, so a design and the app it becomes
use the same values. Each colour has a lighting name that says what it's for,
like the lights on a stage.

## Lighting names

Use the lighting name in briefs and design files, and the Roadie step in code.

| Name      | Roadie step | What it lights                                   |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Flood     | Neutral 0   | Pure white backgrounds, and text on dark ones    |
| Wash      | 2           | Subtle backgrounds, rarely used                  |
| House     | 5           | Patterns, text blocks, and container backgrounds |
| Stage     | 7           | Secondary actions, links, and borders            |
| Spotlight | 9           | Primary calls to action and hero moments         |
| Backstage | 11          | Secondary text                                   |
| Blackout  | 12          | Body text, headings, and dark backgrounds        |

## Swatches

Each card shows a colour's light value, which brand artwork keeps in dark mode
too. Select a token, hex, or CMYK value to copy it, and a message confirms
what you copied. CMYK marked derived is a
screen-to-print equivalent, converted from the hex through a coated press
profile, to confirm with a proof.

### Neutral

Neutral carries most text and backgrounds. Blackout is for body text and
headings, and Backstage for secondary text.

The core brand colour, for trust, reliability, and partnership. In code, it's

## Pairing colours

Lead with blue, and let one spotlight colour be the hero of a piece.

  <Guideline.Dont

  </Guideline.Dont>

- Keep bright colours off large backgrounds. The pattern is the exception,
  with house colour curves on a spotlight ground.
- Keep blue in every Oztix logo and primary call to action.
- In print, use house colours for large areas, specify CMYK, and test on the
  actual paper stock.

## Contrast

Contrast is measured with APCA, at the minimums on
[Colours](/foundations/colors#contrast): Lc 75 for body text, Lc 60 for labels
on strong fills and large text, and Lc 45 for non-text elements. White text on
Spotlight blue passes for labels and large text. Never use colour alone to
carry meaning.

## In Roadie

- Emphasis picks the step, so code names the intent rather than a lighting
  name. See [Colours](/foundations/colors).
- Artwork that keeps its light colours in dark mode uses the pinned `-light-`
  tokens, such as `--color-brand-blue-light-9`, which exist for steps 5, 9, 12,
  and 13, and neutral 0. The swatch tokens follow the theme.
- Every step of every scale is on [Color scales](/tokens/color-scales).
