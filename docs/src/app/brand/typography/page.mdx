Oztix sets everything in Intermission, from partnership proposals to social
posts. One typeface used the same way everywhere builds trust and recognition.

## Typeface

Intermission is Oztix's version of [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/), with
features such as single-storey a, open digits, and round quotes on by default.
Roadie loads it for every app, and the
[font files](https://github.com/TicketSolutionsPtyLtd/roadie/tree/main/packages/core/src/fonts/full)
are there for design tools. Where it can't load, text falls back to Inter,
then the system's sans serif.

## Hierarchy

Roadie's prose text styles are the brand's hierarchy. Large, heavy headings
lead, and lighter weights carry the supporting copy.

```tsx-live layout=stack gap=3
<h1 className='text-display-prose-1 text-strong'>Built on partnerships</h1>
<h2 className='text-display-prose-3 text-strong'>Great events need great partners</h2>
<p className='text-prose font-medium text-strong'>Festivals, tour runs, and concerts</p>
<p className='text-prose'>
  Access to Australia's largest event database and marketing expertise that
  actually drives attendance.
</p>
```

| Weight        | Use for                                             | Roadie style                    |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Black (900)   | Campaign headers, event titles, and covers          | `text-display-prose-1`          |
| Bold (700)    | Section headings, key messages, and calls to action | `text-display-prose-2` to `-6`  |
| Medium (500)  | Subtitles, captions, dates, venues, and speakers    | `text-prose` with `font-medium` |
| Regular (400) | Body copy and descriptions                          | `text-prose`                    |

Brand and marketing work uses only these four weights. Product interfaces also
use Semibold, for small headings.

### Print sizes

Print sets the same weights at fixed minimum sizes. In a deck, slide titles
are Black and section headers Bold.

| Format              | Black | Bold | Regular |
| ------------------- | ----- | ---- | ------- |
| Large format        | 48pt  | 24pt | 18pt    |
| Business collateral | 28pt  | 18pt | 14pt    |

## Text blocks

A text block sets a key message on a colour, so it stands out. The highlight
block is Roadie's `Mark`, which fills with the house colour of its intent.

```tsx-live layout=stack gap=3
<h2 className='text-display-prose-2 text-strong'>
  <Mark intent='brand-blue'>You sell more tickets</Mark>
</h2>
<h2 className='text-display-prose-2 text-strong'>
  <Mark intent='brand-purple'>Your events run smoothly</Mark>
</h2>
<h2 className='text-display-prose-2 text-strong'>
  <Mark intent='brand-orange'>You grow year after year</Mark>
</h2>
```

Choose the colour by the content's energy, and pad a block twice as much at
the sides as at the top and bottom.

<PairTable>

| Colour       | Content                                                    |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flood        | Clean information blocks, paired with another block colour |
| House blue   | Neutral, trustworthy content                               |
| House purple | Partnership announcements and community content            |
| House orange | Action, calls to action, and urgent information            |

## Guidelines

Sentence case and real hierarchy make Oztix copy easy to read at a glance.

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Sentence case, not capitals'>
  <Guideline.Do example={<p className='text-display-prose-3 text-strong'>Great events need great partners</p>}>
    Sentence case for every heading and button. The tagline, Built on
    Partnerships, is the one exception.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont example={<p className='text-display-prose-3 text-strong'>GREAT EVENTS NEED GREAT PARTNERS</p>}>
    No all caps, even for small labels. Acronyms are the only exception.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

- Keep body text at 16px or larger on screen and 14pt or larger in print, and
  nothing smaller than 12px anywhere.
- Use at most three heading levels in one piece, and keep social headlines
  under six words.
- Leave enough contrast over any background, at the minimums on
  [Colour](/brand/colour#contrast).
- In co-branded work, keep the Oztix hierarchy for Oztix content. A partner's
  font is fine in the partner's own section.
