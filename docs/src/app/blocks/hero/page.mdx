Opens a page with its title, as the page's main heading.

## Import

```tsx
import { Hero } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/hero'
```

## Examples

### Bento

The page hero for landing pages. The title sits over a photo, beside a
muted, looping video. Once the block is about 36rem wide, `secondaryImages`
fill the panel beside the video, and from about 56rem, `primaryImages` sit
beside the title. Each set of photos crossfades while it's on screen. A
button in the corner pauses and plays the video and photos together, and
they start paused for viewers who reduce motion.

```tsx-live-expand id=bento
<Hero
  preset='bento'
  title='Australia’s ticketing partner'
  titleHighlight='Australia’s'
  description='Australian owned and hosted, nationwide 7 days a week. Built on partnerships, powered by people and service.'
  actions={[
    { label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact/sales' },
    { label: 'Create my event', href: 'https://studio.oztix.com.au' }
  ]}
  backgroundImage={{ src: '/brand/photography/08.webp', alt: '', width: 1280, height: 850 }}
  video={{
    src: '/blocks/hero-reel.mp4',
    poster: { src: '/brand/photography/01.webp', alt: 'Highlights from Velvet Dingo Fest', width: 1280, height: 854 }
  }}
  primaryImages={[
    { src: '/brand/photography/03.webp', alt: 'Two Oztix team members working at their desks', width: 1280, height: 1007 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/04.webp', alt: 'Event staff scanning tickets at the gate', width: 1280, height: 1007 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/11.webp', alt: 'Three Oztix team members on a couch', width: 1280, height: 911 }
  ]}
  secondaryImages={[
    { src: '/brand/photography/12.webp', alt: 'A crowd in a laneway strung with bunting', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/07.webp', alt: 'Guests on the terrace at Lighthouse Fig Lawn', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/06.webp', alt: 'A panel talking on stage at Ochre Kite Weekender', width: 1280, height: 854 }
  ]}
/>
```

### Image

A title beside one photo, for industry and service pages. Once the block is
about 36rem wide, the text and photo sit side by side, and from about 56rem
the text takes two fifths. Below that, the photo sits above the text. The
photo keeps its own shape.

```tsx-live-expand id=image
<Hero
  preset='image'
  title='Sell more tickets with Spotify'
  titleHighlight='Spotify'
  description='Your events show on artist profiles, so fans find them where they already listen.'
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact/sales' }]}
  image={{ src: '/brand/photography/10.webp', alt: 'A band posing in front of an Oztix backdrop', width: 1280, height: 834 }}
/>
```

### Title

An eyebrow and a large title, for a page that opens on its text.

```tsx-live id=title
<Hero
  preset='title'
  eyebrow='Our mission'
  title='We empower venues and event organisers to create thriving events and communities through genuine partnerships.'
/>
```

### Intent and emphasis

`intent` colours the highlighted words and the strong action, and in Bento
the tint over the photo too. Without one, the highlight is brand-secondary,
the action accent, and the tint brand. The hero has no `emphasis`: its look
comes from its preset.

```tsx-live id=intent-and-emphasis
<Hero
  preset='title'
  intent='brand-purple'
  eyebrow='Festivals'
  title='Ticketing built for festival weekends'
  titleHighlight='festival weekends'
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact/sales' }]}
/>
```

```tsx-live-expand id=intent-and-emphasis-image
<Hero
  preset='image'
  intent='success'
  title='Tickets for every community event'
  titleHighlight='community'
  description='Low fees and local support for not-for-profit organisers.'
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact/sales' }]}
  image={{ src: '/brand/photography/07.webp', alt: 'Guests on the terrace at Lighthouse Fig Lawn', width: 1280, height: 854 }}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Say what the page offers in the title, such as “Australia’s ticketing
    partner”, and back it up in one sentence.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Open with a slogan that makes the reader scroll to learn what the page is.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='One hero, first on the page'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Start the page with the hero. Its title is the page’s `h1`.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Put a second hero lower down. Use a block with an `h2` instead.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset by its media'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use Bento on a landing page with a background photo, a video, and at
    least two photos for each cycling panel, such as people and events. Use
    Image for an industry or service page with one strong photo, and Title
    for a page that opens on its text, such as About.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Use Bento without its photos, which leaves panels empty.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Describe the photos that carry meaning'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Give the cycling photos, and the video’s poster, `alt` text that says
    what they show.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Describe the background photo. It’s decoration, so the hero drops its
    `alt`.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Change the intent for a page’s theme, not for variety'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set `intent` when the page belongs to a part of Oztix with its own colour,
    such as a purple festival section.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Give each hero a different intent to make pages look different. Keep
    status intents, such as danger, for status.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Mark one or two words'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Mark the word the title turns on with `titleHighlight`.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>Mark a whole phrase.</Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>
