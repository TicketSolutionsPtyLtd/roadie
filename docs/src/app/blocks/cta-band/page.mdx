Closes a page with a short pitch and the next step to take.

## Import

```tsx
import { CTABand } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/cta-band'
```

## Examples

### With photo

The pitch and actions sit beside a tilted photo that breaks out of the top of the panel. On a phone, the photo sits above the copy.

```tsx-live-expand id=with-photo
<CTABand
  preset='with-photo'
  title='Explore an Oztix partnership'
  titleHighlight='partnership'
  description='We’re always excited to welcome new partners into the Oztix community.'
  body={
    <>
      <p>Let’s start with a get-to-know-you conversation, so we can understand your goals. Then we’ll show you a growth plan that suits your events.</p>
      <p>Ready to move fast? We’re on it.</p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[
    { label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' },
    { label: 'Create my event', href: 'https://studio.oztix.com.au' }
  ]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
```

### Centred

The heading, copy, and actions are centred in the panel, with no photo.

```tsx-live-expand id=centred
<CTABand
  preset='centred'
  title='Talk to our team'
  body={<p>Tell us about your venue or festival, and we’ll find the setup that sells the most tickets.</p>}
  actions={[
    { label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' },
    { label: 'See pricing', href: '/pricing' }
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the page'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `with-photo` to close a page that sells Oztix, with a photo of people at an event.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `centred` where a photo would compete with the page, such as after an FAQ.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Open the body with what happens when someone acts, such as a call with our team.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don’t spend the body on why the action matters before saying what it is.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep it short'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Write a paragraph or two and at most two actions. The first is the strong one.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>
