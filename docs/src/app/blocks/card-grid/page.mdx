Like items, such as benefits, services, values, or people, as cards in columns or alternating rows.

## Import

```tsx
import { CardGrid } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/card-grid'
```

## Examples

Each preset takes the same `items`, and shows the parts of an item its look uses. Give an item an `href` and its title links to it, with the whole card to click. `actions` adds links under the cards. Columns follow the block's own width, so a card grid in a narrow layout stacks as it would on a phone.

## Guidelines

| Preset       | Suits                                                                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `illustrated-cards`   | Three benefits of one offer, each with a photo and an illustration                     |
| `marked-cards`   | Up to four services or pillars, each with a short subtitle                             |
| `overlap-cards` | Who a page is for, in multiples of three, each with a photo and a line of examples     |
| `numbered-cards`      | How something works, in three or four numbered steps                                   |
| `link-cards`      | Places to go next, such as contact options, each card a link                           |
| `alternating-rows` | A few statements that need a sentence or two each, such as values or the steps of a story |
| `portraits`  | People, such as the team, speakers, or artists, by name and role with a square photo   |
| `lists`      | Two to four short lists that need no images, such as policies or certifications        |

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title each card with the answer, such as "You sell more tickets", and let the description back it up.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't title a card with a teaser, such as "Why it matters", that makes readers open the description.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep the cards alike'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Give every item the same parts, at about the same length, so the cards line up.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't mix a card with an image and a card without one in a preset that shows images.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Card grid reports a `view` once half of it is on screen, and a `click` on a card or an action, labelled with the card's title or the action's label.
