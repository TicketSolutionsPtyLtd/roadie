Shows the organisations behind Oztix, as logo tiles over their photos.

## Import

```tsx
import { Logos } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/logos'
```

## Examples

### Partner tiers

Each partner is a tile with its photo behind, its logo, and its level marked
underneath. Featured partners span two columns at half height, and the tiles
pack densely, so a square tile fills the gap a wide one leaves. An `also`
tile closes the grid with the remaining logos.

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the block with what the partnerships are, such as "Industry
    partnerships", and say in the description what they do.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with a slogan the logos have to explain.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Feature the few that lead'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Feature the presenting and major partners, about one in four tiles, so
    the wide tiles read as the top tier.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't feature every partner; the grid loses its tiers and its rhythm.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Give every tile a light logo and a photo'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use a light or white logo on a transparent background over a photo from
    the partner's event. The photo is decorative and isn't read out, so the
    logo's alt text names the partner.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put a dark logo on a tile; it sinks into the shade behind it. Dark
    logos belong in the `also` tile, which stays light in both themes.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Logos reports a `view` once half of it is on screen. It holds no links, so
it reports no clicks.
