Shows the people and events behind a page in photos.

## Import

```tsx
import { Gallery } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/gallery'
```

## Examples

The default. A heading with previous and next controls over portrait photos that people step through, or swipe on a touch screen.

`intent` colours the highlighted words and the marquee's pause control. The gallery draws no surface, so it takes no emphasis.

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Show people and events'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use photos of crowds, artists, and partners at their events, which crop well to a tall portrait tile.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don’t put logos in the gallery. Logos are a still list in the Logos block.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the gallery with what the photos prove, such as who already trusts Oztix.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Describe every photo'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Write alt text that says what’s in the photo, since the block names each one once to screen readers.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Match the intent to the page'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Leave the intent unset on most pages. Set it to match a campaign page's intent, so the highlight and pause control follow.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Accessibility

- **Carousel**: the heading names the carousel, and each photo is a slide named by its place, such as "2 of 7". Previous and next are buttons, and photos out of view are inert, so `Tab` skips them.
- **Marquee**: each photo is read out once, though the marquee repeats them to fill the width. A pause button stops the motion, which also stops on hover, on focus, and under reduced motion.

## Tracking

Reports a view once half the gallery is on screen, and a click on the carousel's previous or next, or the marquee's pause, with its label.
