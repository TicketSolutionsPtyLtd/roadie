Shows the people, events, and partners behind a page in photos or logos.

## Import

```tsx
import { Gallery } from '@oztix/roadie-blocks/gallery'
```

## Examples

### Carousel

The default. A heading over portrait photos that people step through with the previous, dot, and next controls under them, or swipe on a touch screen.

### Logos

Logos in a wrapping, centred row, each at its own proportions at one height,
so none is cropped. Use it for partner logos or certifications. Give a logo an
`href` and it becomes the link, named by its alt text, as a quiet card that
tints on hover.

```tsx-live-expand id=logos
<Gallery
  preset='logos'
  title='Trusted by organisers across Australia'
  images={[
    { src: '/blocks/kerosene-pony-collective-badge.svg', alt: 'Kerosene Pony Collective', width: 214, height: 48, href: '/partners' },
    { src: '/blocks/wishbone-ferret-collective-badge.svg', alt: 'Wishbone Ferret Collective', width: 214, height: 48 },
    { src: '/blocks/hairpin-bicycle-presents-badge.svg', alt: 'Hairpin Bicycle Presents', width: 216, height: 50 },
    { src: '/blocks/thimble-orbit-presents-badge.svg', alt: 'Thimble Orbit Presents', width: 214, height: 48 }
  ]}
/>
```

### Ratio

`ratio` sets the photos' shape in a `carousel` or `marquee`: `portrait` (9:16)
by default, `square`, or `landscape` (2:1). `logos` ignores it.

```tsx-live-expand id=ratio
<Gallery
  preset='carousel'
  ratio='landscape'
  title='Events that run with Oztix'
  images={[
    { src: '/brand/photography/01.webp', alt: 'A festival main stage under a blue sky, with a crowd in front of it', width: 1280, height: 854 },
```

### Intent and emphasis

`intent` colours the highlighted words, the marquee's pause control, and the strong action. Without an `emphasis`, the photos sit on the page and the marquee runs the block's full width. `subtler`, `subtle`, `normal`, or `raised` draws a full-width band behind them, with the carousel and marquee running edge to edge, and `contained` draws it as a rounded panel inside the page's padding instead.

`align` overrides the heading's alignment, which is centred in every preset. `size='md'` or `'sm'` sets the title one or two steps smaller with tighter spacing, and `actions` go under the photos, lined up with the heading.

```tsx-live-expand id=intent-and-emphasis layout=stack gap=8
{/* Brand, subtle band, action */}
<Gallery
  preset='marquee'
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='subtle'
  title='Our people'
  highlight='people'
  actions={[{ label: 'Join the team', href: '/careers' }]}
  images={[
    { src: '/brand/photography/01.webp', alt: 'A festival main stage under a blue sky, with a crowd in front of it', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/05.webp', alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/12.webp', alt: 'A real crowd in a laneway under Oztix bunting', width: 1280, height: 854 }
  ]}
/>
{/* Normal panel, start-aligned, small */}
<Gallery
  preset='carousel'
  emphasis='normal'
  contained
  align='start'
  size='md'
  title='Events our partners run'
  images={[
    { src: '/brand/photography/07.webp', alt: 'A leafy beer garden full of people in the afternoon sun', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/02.webp', alt: 'A car doing a burnout in blue and pink smoke, in front of a riverside crowd', width: 1280, height: 854 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/08.webp', alt: 'A huge festival crowd facing a main stage, seen from above', width: 1280, height: 850 },
    { src: '/brand/photography/09.webp', alt: 'Four musicians posing together in front of an Oztix backdrop', width: 1201, height: 801 }
  ]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Match the preset to the images'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `carousel` or `marquee` for photos of crowds, artists, and partners at their events, and `logos` for logos, so no logo is cut off.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don’t put logos in a carousel or marquee, or mix photos and logos in one gallery.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the gallery with what the photos prove, such as who already trusts Oztix.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Describe every photo'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Write alt text that says what’s in the photo, since the block names each one once to screen readers.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Match the intent to the page'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Leave the intent unset on most pages. Set it to match a campaign page's intent, so the highlight and pause control follow.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Accessibility

- **Carousel**: the heading names the carousel, and each photo is a slide named by its place, such as "2 of 7". Previous, next, and each dot are buttons, and photos out of view are inert, so `Tab` skips them.
- **Marquee**: each photo is read out once, though the marquee repeats them to fill the width. A pause button stops the motion, which also stops on hover, on focus, and under reduced motion.
- **Logos**: the logos are a list, read in order.
- **Links**: a linked image is the link, named by its alt text, so give every linked image alt text.

## Tracking

Reports a view once half the gallery is on screen, and a click on the carousel's previous, next, or a dot, the marquee's pause, or a linked image, with its label.
