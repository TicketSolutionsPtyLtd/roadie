Logos in a grid, as photo tiles with a label or as a strip of logos alone.

## Import

```tsx
import { Mosaic } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/mosaic'
```

## Examples

### Tiers

Each partner is a tile with its photo behind, its logo, and its level as the
tile's `description`. Tiles with `size: 'lg'` span two columns at half height, and the tiles
pack densely, so a square tile fills the gap a wide one leaves. A `logoTile`
closes the grid with the remaining logos, titled "Also supporting" unless
you give it a title.

### Logo strip

A wrapping, centred row of logos at one height, with no photos, for partner
logos or certifications. It reads each tile's `logo`, or shows its `title`
where it has none, and leaves out photos, descriptions, sizes, and the logo
tile.

```tsx-live id=logo-strip
<Mosaic
  preset='logo-strip'
  title='Trusted by organisers across Australia'
  tiles={[
    { title: 'Kerosene Pony Collective', logo: { src: '/blocks/kerosene-pony-collective-badge.svg', alt: 'Kerosene Pony Collective', width: 240, height: 80 } },
    { title: 'Wishbone Ferret Collective', logo: { src: '/blocks/wishbone-ferret-collective-badge.svg', alt: 'Wishbone Ferret Collective', width: 240, height: 80 } },
    { title: 'Hairpin Bicycle Presents', logo: { src: '/blocks/hairpin-bicycle-presents-badge.svg', alt: 'Hairpin Bicycle Presents', width: 240, height: 80 } },
    { title: 'Thimble Orbit Presents', logo: { src: '/blocks/thimble-orbit-presents-badge.svg', alt: 'Thimble Orbit Presents', width: 240, height: 80 } }
  ]}
/>
```

### Intent and emphasis

Mosaic takes an `intent`, which its tile descriptions, highlighted words, and
strong action follow; without one, descriptions are neutral and highlights
brand-secondary. A tile's own `intent` marks its description in place of the
block's. `emphasis` draws a `subtler`, `subtle`, `normal`, or `raised` band behind the
block, or with `contained`, a rounded panel inside the page's padding. `align`
overrides the heading's alignment, which starts for tiers and centres for a
logo strip, and `size='md'` or `'sm'` sets the title one or two steps smaller with tighter
spacing. `actions` go under the tiles. Give a tile an `href` and its title or
logo becomes the link, with the whole tile as the hit area and its
photo easing larger on hover; tiles without one stay still. A linked strip
logo is a quiet card that tints on hover.

```tsx-live id=intent-and-emphasis
<Mosaic
  preset='tiers'
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='subtle'
  actions={[{ label: 'Become a partner', href: '/partners' }]}
  title='Festival partners'
  titleHighlight='partners'
  tiles={[
    {
      title: 'Ochre Kite Weekender',
      description: 'Presenting partner',
      href: '/partners/ochre-kite-weekender',
      logo: { src: '/blocks/ochre-kite-weekender.svg', alt: 'Ochre Kite Weekender logo', width: 180, height: 60 },
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/01.webp', alt: '', width: 800, height: 800 },
      size: 'lg'
    },
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Keep the defaults unless the page has a reason'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set an `intent` when the page's own colour runs through it, such as a
    brand campaign, and give a tile an `href` when it has its own page.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't change the intent block by block to add variety; the labels then
    read as meaning something.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the content'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use tiers, as partner tiers are today, for partners and sponsors with a
    level each, and logo strip for a row of partner logos or certifications.
    Venues, events, or clients in a mosaic would be new presets, raised as a
    block request.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put venues or events in tiers; their labels aren't levels.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the block with what the partnerships are, such as "Industry
    partnerships", and say in the description what they do.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with a slogan the logos have to explain.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Make the few that lead large'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set the presenting and major partners to `size: 'lg'`, about one in four
    tiles, so the wide tiles read as the top tier.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't make every partner large; the grid loses its tiers and its rhythm.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Match the logo to its surface'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use a light or white logo on a transparent background over a photo from
    the partner's event. The photo is decorative and isn't read out, so the
    logo's alt text names the partner.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put a dark logo on a tiers tile; it sinks into the shade behind it.
    Dark logos belong in the `logoTile`, which stays light in both themes,
    and logo strip logos need their own background, since the page's
    changes with the theme.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Mosaic reports a `view` once half of it is on screen. It holds no links, so
it reports no clicks.
