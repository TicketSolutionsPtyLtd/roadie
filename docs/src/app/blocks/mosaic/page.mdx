A dense grid of photo tiles, each with a logo and a label, some twice as wide.

## Import

```tsx
import { Mosaic } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/mosaic'
```

## Examples

### Partner tiers

Each partner is a tile with its photo behind, its logo, and its level as the
tile's `label`. Featured tiles span two columns at half height, and the tiles
pack densely, so a square tile fills the gap a wide one leaves. A `logoTile`
closes the grid with the remaining logos, titled "Also supporting" unless
you give it a title.

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the content'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use partner tiers, today's only preset, for partners and sponsors with a
    level each. Venues, events, or clients in a mosaic would be new presets,
    raised as a block request.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put venues or events in partner tiers; their labels aren't levels.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the block with what the partnerships are, such as "Industry
    partnerships", and say in the description what they do.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with a slogan the logos have to explain.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Feature the few that lead'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Feature the presenting and major partners, about one in four tiles, so
    the wide tiles read as the top tier.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't feature every partner; the grid loses its tiers and its rhythm.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Give every tile a light logo and a photo'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use a light or white logo on a transparent background over a photo from
    the partner's event. The photo is decorative and isn't read out, so the
    logo's alt text names the partner.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put a dark logo on a tile; it sinks into the shade behind it. Dark
    logos belong in the `logoTile`, which stays light in both themes.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Mosaic reports a `view` once half of it is on screen. It holds no links, so
it reports no clicks.
