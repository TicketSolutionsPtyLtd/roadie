A grid of photo tiles that adds visual interest, the large ones first, such
as partners, venues, or event types. Each tile has a logo or title and a
marked label. For a row of logos alone, use a [Gallery](/blocks/gallery) with
`preset='logos'`.

## Import

```tsx
import { Mosaic } from '@oztix/roadie-blocks/mosaic'
```

## Examples

### Tiles

Each tile has its photo behind, a logo or its `title`, and a marked label as
its `description`, such as a partner's level. Tiles with `size: 'lg'` span two columns at half height, and the tiles
pack densely, so a square tile fills the gap a wide one leaves. A `logoTile`
closes the grid with the remaining logos, titled "Also supporting" unless
you give it a title.

### Intent and emphasis

Mosaic takes an `intent`, which its tile descriptions, highlighted words, and
strong action follow; without one, descriptions are neutral and highlights
brand-secondary. A tile's own `intent` marks its description in place of the
block's. `emphasis` draws a `subtler`, `subtle`, `normal`, or `raised` band behind the
block, or with `contained`, a rounded panel inside the page's padding. `align='center'`
centres the heading, which starts by default, and `size='md'` or `'sm'` sets the title one or two steps smaller with tighter
spacing. `actions` go under the tiles. Give a tile an `href` and its title or
logo becomes the link, with the whole tile as the hit area and its
photo easing larger on hover; tiles without one stay still.

```tsx-live id=intent-and-emphasis
<Mosaic
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='subtle'
  actions={[{ label: 'Find a venue', href: '/venues' }]}
  title='Venues we ticket'
  highlight='Venues'
  tiles={[
    {
      title: 'Kazoo Hollow Room',
      description: 'Brisbane',
      href: '/venues/kazoo-hollow-room',
      logo: { src: '/blocks/kazoo-hollow-room.svg', alt: 'Kazoo Hollow Room logo', width: 214, height: 48 },
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/07.webp', alt: '', width: 800, height: 800 },
      size: 'lg'
    },
    {
      title: 'Echidna Gaslight Theatre',
      description: 'Melbourne',
      href: '/venues/echidna-gaslight-theatre',

```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Keep the defaults unless the page has a reason'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set an `intent` when the page's own colour runs through it, such as a
    brand campaign, and give a tile an `href` when it has its own page.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't change the intent block by block to add variety; the labels then
    read as meaning something.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the content'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use a mosaic where a page needs photos to break up its text, with one
    kind of thing per mosaic, such as partners labelled by level or venues by
    city. For a row of partner logos or certifications, use a Gallery with
    `preset='logos'`.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't mix partners, venues, and events in one mosaic; the labels then
    stop meaning one thing.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Title the block with what the tiles are, such as "Industry
    partnerships", and say in the description what they do.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with a slogan the logos have to explain.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Make the few that lead large'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set the tiles to lead with, such as presenting partners, to `size: 'lg'`,
    about one in four tiles, so the wide tiles read first.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't make every tile large; the grid loses its rhythm.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Match the logo to its surface'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use a light or white logo on a transparent background over a photo of
    the venue or event. The photo is decorative and isn't read out, so the
    logo's alt text, or the tile's title, names it.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put a dark logo on a tile or in the `logoTile`; both stay dark in
    either theme, so a dark logo sinks into them.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Mosaic reports a `view` once half of it is on screen, and a click on each
linked tile or action, with its label and href.
