Text beside an image, a partner lockup, or the numbers that back it up.

## Import

```tsx
import { Split } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/split'
```

## Examples

### Feature row

An image beside a heading and short description, on a tinted panel. The
image comes first, and the text wraps below it on narrow screens.

```tsx-live
<Split
  preset='feature-row'
  title='Specialist teams powering venues and event organisers'
  description='Behind our leadership are teams in innovation, operations, marketing, events, content, and finance, all working to deliver on our partnership promise.'
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/03.webp',
    alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
```

### Text and stats

Long-form text at a readable measure, with the numbers that back it up
beside it. The stats wrap below the text on narrow screens. They draw the
same way as the [Stats](/blocks/stats) block.

```tsx-live
<Split
  preset='text-and-stats'
  title='Who we are'
  body={
    <>
      <h3>Australia's largest independent ticketing company</h3>
      <p>
        For over 20 years, we've partnered with event organisers to deliver
        tickets across music, venues, shows, sports, and government events
        nationwide.
      </p>
      <h3>Australian owned and operated</h3>
      <p>
        Our headquarters are in Brisbane, and our platform is hosted in
        Australia, built and improved in-house by our full-time team.
      </p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Meet the team', href: '/about' }]}
  stats={{
    title: 'By the numbers',
    description: 'The impact of genuine partnerships',
    items: [
      { value: '33,000,000+', label: 'tickets sold', illustration: 'Ticket' },
      { value: '22,000+', label: 'event organisers', illustration: 'MapPin' },
      { value: '20+', label: 'years in ticketing', illustration: 'Heart' },
      { value: '50+', label: 'full-time team', illustration: 'HighFive' }
    ]
  }}
/>
```

### Partner spotlight

Opens a partner page: the Oztix logo and the partner's logo joined by a
heart, beside the page title, text, and action. Its title is the page's
`h1`, so it's always the first block on the page.

```tsx-live
<Split
  preset='partner-spotlight'
  title='Resell my tickets'
  description='Sell your Oztix tickets through our resale partner.'
  body={
    <>
      <p>The safest way to resell tickets to any event you can't make.</p>
      <p>
        <strong>Only real tickets for real fans.</strong>
      </p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Resell my tickets', href: 'https://www.example.com' }]}
  partnerLogo={{
    src: '/blocks/partner-logo.svg',
    alt: 'Partner logo',
    width: 208,
    height: 60
  }}
/>
```

## Guidelines

- **Answer first.** Open the text with a sentence that answers the page's
  question, such as what the partnership lets someone do, then back it up.
- **Feature row** suits a short point with a photo that shows it, such as the
  teams behind a service. Keep it to a heading and one or two sentences.
- **Text and stats** suits an about page: a few short sections under `h3`
  headings, with three to six numbers that prove them. Write each value as it
  should read, such as `7 million+`.
- **Partner spotlight** opens a page about one partner. Use the partner's own
  logo artwork, at its natural width, and one action that goes to them.

## Tracking

Split reports a view once it's half on screen, and a click on each action,
with its preset and position.
