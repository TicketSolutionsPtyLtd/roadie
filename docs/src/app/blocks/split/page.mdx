Text beside an image or the numbers that back it up, or rows of text and images that alternate sides.

## Import

```tsx
import { Split } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/split'
```

## Examples

### Media and text

An image beside the heading, text, and actions. The image comes first, cropped
to 4 by 3, and the text wraps below it on narrow screens.

```tsx-live-expand id=media-text
<Split
  preset='media-text'
  title='Sell more tickets with your partners'
  body={
    <p>
      List your events where your audience already spends time, and every sale
      still lands in your Oztix reports.
    </p>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
```

Give it `stats` in place of an image to set long-form text at a readable
measure, with the numbers that back it up beside it. The stats wrap below the
text on narrow screens, and draw the same way as the [Stats](/blocks/stats)
block.

```tsx-live-expand id=text-and-stats
<Split
  preset='media-text'
  title='Who we are'
  body={
    <>
      <h3>Australia's largest independent ticketing company</h3>
      <p>
        For over 20 years, we've partnered with event organisers to deliver
        tickets across music, venues, shows, sports, and government events
        nationwide.
      </p>
      <h3>Australian owned and operated</h3>
      <p>
        Our headquarters are in Brisbane, and our platform is hosted in
        Australia, built and improved in-house by our full-time team.
      </p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Meet the team', href: '/about' }]}
  stats={{
    title: 'By the numbers',
    description: 'The impact of genuine partnerships',
    items: [
      { value: '33,000,000+', label: 'tickets sold', illustration: 'Ticket' },
      { value: '22,000+', label: 'event organisers', illustration: 'MapPin' },
      { value: '20+', label: 'years in ticketing', illustration: 'Heart' },
      { value: '50+', label: 'full-time team', illustration: 'HighFive' }
    ]
  }}
/>
```

### Alternating rows

Rows of a marked title and description beside a square image, alternating
sides, starting with the image on the left. Each row's title is an `h3`
under the block's heading, and the images wrap below the text on phones.

```tsx-live-expand id=alternating-rows
<Split
  preset='alternating-rows'
  title='Our values'
  description='Five principles shape how we partner with you.'
  items={[
    {
      title: 'Proactive touch',
      subtitle: 'See it coming, solve it early',
      description: 'We spot issues before they become problems, and opportunities before you need to ask.',
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/03.webp', alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors', width: 1280, height: 1007 }
    },
    {
      title: 'Positive energy',
      subtitle: 'Lift spirits, spark connections',
      description: 'We love what we do, and that flows through to your team and your customers.',
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/05.webp', alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event', width: 1280, height: 854 }
    },
    {
      title: 'Integrity and ownership',
      subtitle: 'Own decisions, build trust',
      description: "We do what we say we'll do, and own it when something doesn't work.",
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/11.webp', alt: 'Three people sitting together on a couch in a bar lined with gig posters', width: 1280, height: 911 }
    }
  ]}
/>
```

### Intent and emphasis

`intent` colours the highlights, actions, and marks. `emphasis` sets the
surface behind the block: `subtle`, `subtler`, or `raised`. On a surface, the
title takes the intent and the rest of the text stays neutral. `media-text`
draws its surface as a panel inside the page's padding, and
`alternating-rows` as a full-width band. `itemEmphasis` sets every row's
surface in `alternating-rows`.

```tsx-live-expand id=intent-and-emphasis layout=stack gap=8
{/* Brand, subtle panel */}
<Split
  preset='media-text'
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='subtle'
  title='Sell more tickets with your partners'
  titleHighlight='more tickets'
  body={<p>List your events where your audience already spends time.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
{/* Success, raised band */}
<Split
  preset='alternating-rows'
  intent='success'
  emphasis='raised'
  title='How payouts work'
  items={[
    {
      title: 'Doors close',
      description: 'Settlement starts automatically once your event ends.',
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/04.webp', alt: 'A staff member in a blue vest scanning a ticket on a phone at an event entry', width: 1280, height: 1007 }
    },
    {
      title: 'Money arrives',
      description: 'Your payout lands within days, without chasing it.',
      image: { src: '/brand/photography/03.webp', alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors', width: 1280, height: 1007 }
    }
  ]}
/>
```

### Align, size, and inset

`size='md'` sets the title a step smaller and tightens the gaps. In
`media-text`, it narrows the image to two fifths, cropped 8 by 5, with the
body in subtle text: a short point with a photo, on a tinted panel. `align`
centres the heading, text, and actions, and `inset={false}` runs a surface
full width as a band.

```tsx-live-expand id=align-size-inset layout=stack gap=8
{/* Medium, on a subtle info panel */}
<Split
  preset='media-text'
  size='md'
  intent='info'
  emphasis='subtle'
  title='Specialist teams powering venues and event organisers'
  description='Behind our leadership are teams in innovation, operations, marketing, events, content, and finance, all working to deliver on our partnership promise.'
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/03.webp',
    alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
{/* Centred, on a subtler band */}
<Split
  preset='media-text'
  align='center'
  emphasis='subtler'
  inset={false}
  title='Talk to our partnerships team'
  body={<p>Tell us about your venue or event, and we'll take it from there.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Open the text with a sentence that answers the page's question, such as what the partnership lets someone do, then back it up.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with why the partnership matters to us before saying what it does.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the content'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use media and text for a section beside a photo, at `md` for a short point the photo shows, with stats for an about page with three to six numbers that prove it, and alternating rows for a few statements that need a sentence or two each, such as values.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open a page with Split. Use [Hero](/blocks/hero), whose `partner` preset opens a page about one partner.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Write stats as they should read'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Round each value and write it as it reads, such as `7 million+`, with a label that says what it counts.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't add an illustration that doesn't match its label.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep the defaults unless the page needs a change of pace'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set an intent to match the page's brand or a campaign, or a panel to separate a section from the ones around it.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put every Split on a panel, or give neighbouring blocks different intents. Each change loses its point when everything changes.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking

Split reports a view once it's half on screen, and a click on each action or
linked row, with its preset and position.
