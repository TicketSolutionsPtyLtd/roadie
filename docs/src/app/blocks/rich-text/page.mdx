A title and rich text, from a short statement to an article.

## Import

```tsx
import { RichText } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/rich-text'
```

## Examples

### Statement

A centred illustration, title, and a few large paragraphs, with generous
space around them.

```tsx-live-expand id=statement
<RichText
  preset='statement'
  title='We’re Australian-owned, Australian-hosted, and operate 7 days a week nationwide.'
  body={
    <>
      <p>
        As Australia’s largest independent ticketing company, our mission for
        20+ years remains the same: to create thriving events and communities
        through genuine partnerships that focus on your goals.
      </p>
      <p>
        Our clients range from national brands and independent operators to
        corporate, government, and not-for-profit organisers.
      </p>
    </>
  }
/>
```

### Long form

Long-form text set in `Prose` at a reading width. The title is optional,
and the body can carry its own headings from `h3`.

```tsx-live id=long-form
<RichText
  preset='long-form'
  title='How refunds work'
  body={
    <>
      <p>
        When an event is cancelled, every ticket is refunded to the card it
        was bought with, within 10 business days.
      </p>
      <h3>Booking fees</h3>
      <p>
        Booking fees come back too. If the event is postponed, tickets carry
        over to the new date, and anyone who can't make it can ask for a
        refund.
      </p>
    </>
  }
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Open with the sentence that answers the reader’s question, such as what
    Oztix is.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Lead with the story behind the answer.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep a statement short'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Give a statement one title and two or three paragraphs.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Run a statement long. Longer text is long form.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Start body headings at h3'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `h3` and below inside long form, since the block’s title is the
    `h2`.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>Put an `h1` or `h2` in the body.</Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pass rendered text'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Pass `body` already rendered, such as Portable Text from the CMS. The block
    sets it in `Prose`.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Style the body’s text yourself. Prose sets it.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>
