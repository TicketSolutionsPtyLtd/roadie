A title and rich text, from a short statement to an article.

## Import

```tsx
import { RichText } from '@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/rich-text'
```

## Examples

### Statement

A centred illustration, title, and a few large paragraphs, with generous
space around them.

```tsx-live id=statement -expand
<RichText
  preset='statement'
  title='We’re Australian-owned, Australian-hosted, and operate 7 days a week nationwide.'
  body={
    <>
      <p>
        As Australia’s largest independent ticketing company, our mission for
        20+ years remains the same: to create thriving events and communities
        through genuine partnerships that focus on your goals.
      </p>
      <p>
        Our clients range from national brands and independent operators to
        corporate, government, and not-for-profit organisers.
      </p>
    </>
  }
/>
```

### Article body

Long-form text set in `Prose` at a reading width. The title is optional,
and the body can carry its own headings from `h3`.

```tsx-live id=article-body
<RichText
  preset='article-body'
  title='How refunds work'
  body={
    <>
      <p>
        When an event is cancelled, every ticket is refunded to the card it
        was bought with, within 10 business days.
      </p>
      <h3>Booking fees</h3>
      <p>
        Booking fees come back too. If the event is postponed, tickets carry
        over to the new date, and anyone who can't make it can ask for a
        refund.
      </p>
    </>
  }
/>
```

## Guidelines

- **Answer first.** Open with the sentence that answers the reader's
  question, such as what Oztix is, before the story behind it.
- **Statement for a short belief.** One title and two or three paragraphs.
  Anything longer is an article body.
- **Article body for reading.** Policies, guides, and stories, with headings
  from `h3` inside the body, since the block's title is the `h2`.
- **Rendered text in.** Pass `body` already rendered, such as Portable Text
  from the CMS. The block sets it in `Prose`, so it brings no styles of its
  own.

## Tracking

Reports a view when half the block is on screen, and a click on each link in
the body.
