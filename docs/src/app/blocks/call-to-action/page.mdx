Closes a page with a short pitch and the next step to take. Use one call to
action, last on the page.

## Import

```tsx
import { CallToAction } from '@oztix/roadie-blocks/call-to-action'
```

## Examples

### Photo

The default. The pitch and actions start beside a tilted portrait (3:4) photo that
breaks out of the top of the panel. `ratio='story'` (9:16),
`'square'`, `'landscape'` (3:2), or `'banner'` (2:1) changes its shape. On a phone, the photo sits above the
copy.

```tsx-live-expand id=photo
<CallToAction
  preset='photo'
  title='Explore an Oztix partnership'
  highlight='partnership'
  description='We’re always excited to welcome new partners into the Oztix community.'
  body={
    <>
      <p>Let’s start with a get-to-know-you conversation, so we can understand your goals. Then we’ll show you a growth plan that suits your events.</p>
      <p>Ready to move fast? We’re on it.</p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[
    { label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' },
    { label: 'Create my event', href: 'https://studio.oztix.com.au' }
  ]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
```

`tilted={false}` sets the photo straight, and `illustration` picks the
[spot illustration](/components/spot-illustration) at its corner, `HighFive` by
default.

```tsx-live-expand id=photo-straight
<CallToAction
  preset='photo'
  tilted={false}
  illustration='Ticket'
  title='Talk to a real person'
  body={<p>Our support team answers seven days a week, from people who know your event.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Contact support', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/03.webp',
    alt: 'Two Oztix team members smiling at their desks',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
```

### Centred

The heading, copy, and actions centred in the panel, with no photo.

```tsx-live-expand id=centred
<CallToAction
  preset='centred'
  title='Talk to our team'
  body={<p>Tell us about your venue or festival, and we’ll find the setup that sells the most tickets.</p>}
  actions={[
    { label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' },
    { label: 'See pricing', href: '/pricing' }
  ]}
/>
```

### Intent and emphasis

`intent` tints the panel and the highlighted words, and `emphasis` sets the panel: `raised` by default, or `subtler`, `subtle`, `normal`, `strong`, or `inverted`. On a `strong` or `inverted` panel, the first action switches to an intent that stands apart from the panel's fill, and highlighted words, links, and code take the panel's text colour.

`contained` draws the panel inside the page's padding, rounded at every width, instead of as a full-width band. `align` overrides the preset's alignment, which starts on `photo` and centres on `centred`, and `size='md'` or `'sm'` sets the title one or two steps smaller, with smaller body text and tighter spacing.

```tsx-live-expand id=intent-and-emphasis layout=stack gap=8
{/* Brand, strong */}
<CallToAction
  preset='centred'
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='strong'
  title='List your next event with Oztix'
  body={<p>Set up in minutes and start selling today.</p>}
  actions={[
    { label: 'Create my event', href: 'https://studio.oztix.com.au' },
    { label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' }
  ]}
/>
{/* Inverted */}
<CallToAction
  preset='centred'
  emphasis='inverted'
  title='Talk to our team'
  body={<p>Tell us about your venue, and we’ll find the setup that sells the most tickets.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Contact sales', href: '/contact/sales' }]}
/>
```

```tsx-live-expand id=contained-align-size
<CallToAction
  preset='centred'
  emphasis='subtle'
  intent='info'
  contained
  align='start'
  size='md'
  title='Need a hand with your setup?'
  body={<p>Our support team answers seven days a week.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Contact support', href: '/contact/support' }]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the page'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `photo` to close a page that sells Oztix, with a photo of people at an event.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use `centred` where a photo would compete with the page, such as after an FAQ.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Open the body with what happens when someone acts, such as a call with our team.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don’t spend the body on why the action matters before saying what it is.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep it short'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Write a paragraph or two and at most two actions. The first is the strong one.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep the raised panel'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Keep the raised panel unless the call to action closes a campaign page that needs it to stand out. Use `strong` or `inverted` at most once on a page.
  </Guideline.Do>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>
