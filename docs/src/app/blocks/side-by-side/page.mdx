One section with its own title, text, and buttons beside a photo or a few key numbers. For three to six values or story steps that alternate sides, use [Alternating rows](/blocks/alternating-rows).

## Import

```tsx
import { SideBySide } from '@oztix/roadie-blocks/side-by-side'
```

## Examples

### Image then text

A photo before the heading, text, and actions, cropped to 3 by 2. The text wraps
below it on narrow screens.

```tsx-live-expand id=image-start
<SideBySide
  preset='image-start'
  title='Sell more tickets with your partners'
  body={
    <p>
      List your events where your audience already spends time, and every sale
      still lands in your Oztix reports.
    </p>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
```

### Text then image

`image-end` sets the photo after the text on wide screens, and still above it
on phones. Stack Side by sides that switch between `image-start` and
`image-end` for a page of feature sections, each with its own heading and
actions.

```tsx-live-expand id=image-end
<SideBySide
  preset='image-end'
  title='Your shows, next to the music'
  body={
    <p>
      Your events turn up on artist profiles, in front of fans already
      streaming the artist.
    </p>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'List your event', href: '/create' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/04.webp',
    alt: 'A staff member in a blue vest scanning a ticket on a phone at an event entry',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
```

### Ratio

`ratio` sets the photo's shape in `image-start` and `image-end`: `landscape`
(3 by 2) by default, `square`, `banner` (2 by 1), `portrait` (3 by 4), or
`story` (9 by 16). The photo fills its
shape, so pick the ratio that keeps the subject in frame.

```tsx-live-expand id=ratio
<SideBySide
  preset='image-start'
  ratio='square'
  title='Sell more tickets with your partners'
  body={
    <p>
      List your events where your audience already spends time, and every sale
      still lands in your Oztix reports.
    </p>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
```

### Text and stats

`stats` sets long-form text at a readable measure, with the numbers that back
it up beside it. The stats wrap below the text on narrow screens, and draw the
same way as the [Stats](/blocks/stats) block.

```tsx-live-expand id=text-and-stats
<SideBySide
  preset='stats'
  title='Who we are'
  body={
    <>
      <h3>Australia's largest independent ticketing company</h3>
      <p>
        For over 20 years, we've partnered with event organisers to deliver
        tickets across music, venues, shows, sports, and government events
        nationwide.
      </p>
      <h3>Australian owned and operated</h3>
      <p>
        Our headquarters are in Brisbane, and our platform is hosted in
        Australia, built and improved in-house by our full-time team.
      </p>
    </>
  }
  actions={[{ label: 'Meet the team', href: '/about' }]}
  stats={{
    title: 'By the numbers',
    description: 'The impact of genuine partnerships',
    figures: [
      { value: '33,000,000+', label: 'tickets sold', illustration: 'Ticket' },
      { value: '22,000+', label: 'event organisers', illustration: 'MapPin' },
      { value: '20+', label: 'years in ticketing', illustration: 'Heart' },
      { value: '50+', label: 'full-time team', illustration: 'HighFive' }
    ]
  }}
/>
```

### Intent and emphasis

`intent` colours the highlights and actions. `emphasis` sets the surface
behind the block: `subtle`, `subtler`, `normal`, `raised`, `strong`, or
`inverted`. On a surface, the title takes the intent and the rest of the text
stays neutral. On `strong` or `inverted`, all of it takes the fill's own colour. Side by side draws its
surface as a rounded panel in line with the hero.

```tsx-live-expand id=intent-and-emphasis layout=stack gap=8
{/* Brand, subtle panel */}
<SideBySide
  preset='image-start'
  intent='brand'
  emphasis='subtle'
  title='Sell more tickets with your partners'
  highlight='more tickets'
  body={<p>List your events where your audience already spends time.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/05.webp',
    alt: 'Three partners smiling together at a laneway event',
    width: 1280,
    height: 854
  }}
/>
{/* Success, normal panel */}
<SideBySide
  preset='image-end'
  intent='success'
  emphasis='normal'
  title='Payouts that arrive on time'
  highlight='on time'
  body={<p>Settlement starts once your event ends, and lands within days.</p>}
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/03.webp',
    alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
```

### Align, size, and contained

`size='md'` or `'sm'` sets the title one or two steps smaller and the text a
step smaller, and tightens the gaps. Beside a photo, it narrows the photo to two fifths or a third of the
width, without changing its shape. `align` centres the heading, text, and actions, and
`contained={false}` runs a surface across the window as a band.

```tsx-live-expand id=align-size-contained layout=stack gap=8
{/* Medium, subtle info panel */}
<SideBySide
  preset='image-start'
  size='md'
  intent='info'
  emphasis='subtle'
  title='Specialist teams powering venues and event organisers'
  description='Behind our leadership are teams in innovation, operations, marketing, events, content, and finance, all working to deliver on our partnership promise.'
  image={{
    src: '/brand/photography/03.webp',
    alt: 'Two team members working together at a desk with large monitors',
    width: 1280,
    height: 1007
  }}
/>
{/* Centred, subtler band */}
<SideBySide
  preset='image-start'
  align='center'
  emphasis='subtler'
  contained={false}
  title='Talk to our partnerships team'
  body={<p>Tell us about your venue or event, and we'll take it from there.</p>}
  actions={[{ label: 'Talk to us', href: '/contact' }]}
/>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Answer first'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Open the text with a sentence that answers the page's question, such as what the partnership lets someone do, then back it up.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open with why the partnership matters to us before saying what it does.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick the preset for the content'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Use an image for a section beside a photo, at `md` for a short point the photo shows, and stats for an about page with three to six numbers that prove it. For three to six values or story steps that need a sentence or two each, use [Alternating rows](/blocks/alternating-rows).
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't open a page with Side by side. Use [Hero](/blocks/hero), whose `partner` preset opens a page about one partner.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Write stats as they should read'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Round each value and write it as it reads, such as `7 million+`, with a label that says what it counts.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't add an illustration that doesn't match its label.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep the defaults unless the page needs a change of pace'>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Set an intent to match the page's brand or a campaign, or a panel to separate a section from the ones around it.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Don't put every Side by side on a panel, or give neighbouring blocks different intents. Each change loses its point when everything changes.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Tracking
