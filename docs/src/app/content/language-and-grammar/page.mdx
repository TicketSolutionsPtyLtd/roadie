[Voice and tone](/content/voice-and-tone) sets how Oztix sounds. This page
sets how we write it down, in docs, interfaces, support, and marketing.

## Grammar

### Australian English

Use Australian spelling and terms. Code keeps its own spelling, such as
`color` in CSS.

<PairTable>

| Write                              | Not                                           |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| optimise, theatre, colour, licence | optimize, theater, color, license (as a noun) |

</PairTable>

### Sentence case

Capitalise only the first word and proper nouns, in titles, headings, menu
items, and buttons.

<PairTable>

| Write                                   | Not                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| How to set up your event                | How To Set Up Your Event                |
| Setting up Oztix at your Brisbane venue | Setting up oztix at your brisbane venue |

</PairTable>

### Contractions

Contractions keep the tone natural.

<PairTable>

| Write                | Not                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| you'll, we're, can't | you will, we are, cannot |

</PairTable>

<Guidelines>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Pronouns"
  description={`Use "we" for Oztix, "you" for the reader, and "they" for a person whose gender you don't know.`}
>
  <Guideline.Do>

    We'll refund your tickets.

    Ask the ticket holder to bring their ID.

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>

    Oztix will refund the customer's tickets.

    Ask the ticket holder to bring his or her ID.

  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

### Numbers

Write one to ten as words and 11 and above as numerals. Tickets, prices,
percentages, times, dates, and durations are always numerals.

<PairTable>

| Write                     | Not                         |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| three venues, 12 venues   | 3 venues, twelve venues     |
| 4 tickets, 5%, 1,000 fans | four tickets, five per cent |

</PairTable>

<Guidelines>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Lists"
  description="Keep items parallel and the list to six items or fewer. Use bullets for options and numbers for steps."
>
  <Guideline.Do>
    Add a ticket type. Set its price. Publish the event.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>
    Add a ticket type. Pricing. Then you should publish.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

### Industry terms

Use the reader's market's terms, and explain one the first time it might be
new.

<PairTable>

| Write                                        | Not                                      |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| general admission standing area, sound check | general admin area, audio testing period |
| floor plan, house capacity                   | room layout, maximum people              |

</PairTable>

### Abbreviations

Spell a term out the first time, with the abbreviation in parentheses, then
use the abbreviation. Keep internal abbreviations out of anything a client
reads.

<PairTable>

| Write                                | Not              |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| return on investment (ROI), then ROI | ROI on first use |
| booking fee                          | bf               |

</PairTable>

## Punctuation

### Dashes

No em dashes or en dashes, anywhere. Use a full stop, a comma, or
parentheses. Hyphens join compound words and number ranges. Spans of dates,
times, and prices use "to".

<PairTable>

| Write                                             | Not                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Doors open at 7pm. The support act starts at 8pm. | Doors open at 7pm &mdash; the support act starts at 8pm. |
| 7:30pm to 10:30pm, $50 to $100                    | 7:30pm&ndash;10:30pm, $50&ndash;$100                     |
| rows 1-10, early-bird tickets                     | rows 1&ndash;10, early bird tickets                      |

</PairTable>

<Guidelines>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Colons"
  description="Use a colon before a list or an example, and in times. Never use one in a heading or to join two halves of a sentence."
>
  <Guideline.Do>

    Event planning for first-time promoters

    Bring three things: your ticket, photo ID, and a water bottle.

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>

    Event planning: key considerations

    The result: sales doubled.

  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

### Commas

Use the Oxford comma in every list of three or more. Use commas for thousands,
after a spelled-out weekday, and in addresses. Never put one before the year.

<PairTable>

| Write                             | Not                              |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| tickets, wristbands, and passes   | tickets, wristbands and passes   |
| Friday, 27 November 2026          | Friday 27 November, 2026         |
| 1,000 tickets                     | 1000 tickets                     |
| The Quilted Walrus Room, Brisbane | The Quilted Walrus Room Brisbane |

</PairTable>

### Apostrophes

Use apostrophes for possession and contractions, never for plurals.

<PairTable>

| Write                             | Not           |
| --------------------------------- | ------------- |
| the venue's capacity, VIPs, 1990s | VIP's, 1990's |

</PairTable>

<Guidelines>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Quotation marks"
  description="Use double quotes for a quote and single quotes inside it. Punctuation that belongs to the quote goes inside it, and punctuation that belongs to your sentence goes outside. Never use quotes for emphasis. Code, Markdown, and AI drafts use straight quotes; rendered copy may use typographic quotes."
>
  <Guideline.Do>

    Fans called it "the best night of the year".

    The best seats in the house

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>

    Fans called it "the best night of the year."

    The "best" seats in the house

  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

### Parentheses

Use parentheses for prices, abbreviations, and short asides.

<PairTable>

| Write                             | Not                                     |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Early-bird tickets $40 (save 20%) | Early-bird tickets $40 &mdash; save 20% |

</PairTable>

### Exclamation marks and emoji

Use one exclamation mark at most on a page, for real excitement. Leave out
emoticons, text speak, and decorative emoji in headings, bullets, and
labels.

<PairTable>

| Write      | Not            |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| You're in! | You're in!!! 🎉 |

</PairTable>

<Guidelines>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Bold"
  description="Bold names interface elements, with their exact names and a slash between the steps of a path. Elsewhere, bold rarely, and never every proper noun or acronym."
>
  <Guideline.Do>

    Go to **Event setup** / **Tickets** / **Add ticket type**.

    Go to **Event setup** / **Seating**.

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>

    Click the ticket button in settings.

    Go to the **event setup section** and look for the **seating options**.

  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline
  headingLevel={3}
  title="Full stops"
  description="End every whole sentence with a full stop, including a bullet that is a whole sentence. Headings, interface labels, and fragments take none."
>
  <Guideline.Do>

    Buy tickets

    Enter an email address like name@example.com.

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont>

    Buy tickets.

    Invalid email

  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

### Ellipses

Use an ellipsis only for text an interface truncates, never for drama.

<PairTable>

| Write                  | Not                        |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Sapphire Cassowary Ni… | Something big is coming... |

</PairTable>

### Slashes

Use a slash for a real either-or pair, with no spaces around it. A path
through the interface takes a slash with a space each side, as in Bold.

<PairTable>

| Write                | Not                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| indoor/outdoor venue | indoor / outdoor venue |

</PairTable>

### Ampersands

Write "and". An ampersand is fine only in an official name or a tight
interface label.

<PairTable>

| Write          | Not          |
| -------------- | ------------ |
| Food and drink | Food & drink |

</PairTable>

## Formats

### Dates and times

The [Date and time](/foundations/date-and-time) foundation is the one home for
dates, times, durations, and spans. These are the everyday shapes.

<PairTable>

| Write             | Not                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| Fri 27 Nov 2026   | Fri 27th Nov, Nov 27, 2026 |
| 7:30pm to 10:30pm | 7:30 PM - 10:30 PM         |
| 9:00am AEDT       | 9:00am +11:00              |

</PairTable>

### Prices

Put the dollar sign against the number, show cents only when the amount isn't
round, and use commas for thousands. A range uses "to" with the highest price
last, and a starting price uses "from".

<PairTable>

| Write                 | Not                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------- |
| $50, $19.95, $1,000   | $50.00, $ 50, $1000  |
| $50 to $100, from $30 | $50&ndash;$100, $50+ |

</PairTable>

### Fees and offers

Marketing says the price includes the booking fee. Checkout shows the ticket
price, the booking fee, and the total on their own lines.

<PairTable>

| Write                                                            | Not                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| All prices include booking fee                                   | plus booking fee, inc. bf, + bf |
| Members $40 (save $10), non-members $50                          | $50 ($40 for members)           |
| Early-bird tickets $40 (save 20%) until Friday, 27 November 2026 | Cheap tix for early birds!!     |

</PairTable>

Show prices in this order: member or presale, then regular, then extras such
as VIP and packages, then the booking fee. A package shows its total first,
then what it includes.

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Labels are sentence case with no full stop'>
  <Guideline.Do example={<Button intent='accent' emphasis='strong'>Buy tickets</Button>}>
    Capitalise the first word only, and leave the full stop off a label.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont example={<Button intent='accent' emphasis='strong'>Buy Tickets.</Button>}>
    Don't use title case or end a label with a full stop.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Error text is a whole sentence that says how to fix it'>
  <Guideline.Do
    width={64}
    example={
      <Field invalid>
        <Field.Label>Email</Field.Label>
        <Field.Input defaultValue='sam@example' />
        <Field.ErrorText>Enter an email address like name@example.com.</Field.ErrorText>
      </Field>
    }
  >
    Say what to type, in a sentence with a full stop.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont
    width={64}
    example={
      <Field invalid>
        <Field.Label>Email</Field.Label>
        <Field.Input defaultValue='sam@example' />
        <Field.ErrorText>Invalid input!</Field.ErrorText>
      </Field>
    }
  >
    Don't name the problem without the fix.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## In the interface

- [Button](/components/button) and [Menu](/components/menu) labels are
  sentence case fragments with no full stop.
- [Field](/components/field) helper and error text are whole sentences.
- Date and time components, such as [DateTime](/components/date-time), format
  dates for you, so never build a date string by hand.
