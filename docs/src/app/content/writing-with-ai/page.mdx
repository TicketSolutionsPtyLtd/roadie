An AI draft breaks the same rules as any weak draft, and has a few habits of
its own. This page covers those habits, with a draft line before each edit
and after it. Edit everything else against
[Language and grammar](/content/language-and-grammar) and
[Voice and tone](/content/voice-and-tone), like any other draft.

## Trailing -ing phrases

A clause like "highlighting its commitment" or "ensuring a smooth night",
tacked onto the end of a sentence, claims a meaning nobody checked. Delete it,
or give the fact behind it its own sentence.

| Before                                                                        | After                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| The festival added a second stage, highlighting its commitment to local acts. | The festival added a second stage for local acts.  |
| Gates open at 5pm, ensuring a smooth entry for every fan.                     | Gates open at 5pm, an hour earlier than last year. |

## AI vocabulary

Some words turn up far more often in machine writing than in ours. Use the
plain word instead.

| Write                   | Not                   |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| also                    | additionally          |
| important, or say why   | crucial, pivotal      |
| look at                 | delve into            |
| show                    | showcase, underscore  |
| improve                 | enhance               |
| build                   | foster                |
| get                     | garner                |
| busy, loud, or the fact | vibrant               |
| market                  | landscape, as an idea |
| proof of                | testament to          |
| mix                     | tapestry, interplay   |

| Before                                                        | After                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Let's delve into the vibrant landscape of regional festivals. | Here's how regional festivals sold this year. |

## Not just this, but that

"It's not just X, it's Y" sets up a claim nobody made so it can knock it down.
Say the point.

| Before                                                              | After                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| It's not just a ticketing platform, it's a partner in your success. | We run your ticketing, and we answer the phone on show day. |

## Groups of three

List as many things as there are. A draft pads or trims ideas to three
because three sounds finished.

| Before                                          | After                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Fast, simple, and seamless entry for every fan. | Scanning takes 2 seconds a ticket. |

## Bold labels that repeat the line

A bullet that opens with a bold label and a colon, then says the label again,
is a tell. Write the sentence. A bold name that ends in a full stop and leads
into new detail is fine, as in the second row.

| Before                                                     | After                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `**Performance:** Performance improved across all venues.` | Scanning took 2 seconds a ticket, down from 5.      |
| `**Refunds:** Refunds are handled by our team.`            | `**Refunds.** We refund to the card you paid with.` |

## Curly quotes in drafts

Code, Markdown, and AI drafts use straight quotes, so search and code read
them. Rendered copy may use typographic quotes.

| Before               | After                |
| -------------------- | -------------------- |
| `title=‘Doors open’` | `title='Doors open'` |

## Chatbot phrases

Delete the assistant's side of the conversation, such as "Certainly!", "I hope
this helps!", and "Let me know if you have any questions". A warm sign-off is
fine only after a concrete next step.

| Before                                           | After                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Certainly! Here's the update. I hope this helps! | Gates open at 6pm. I'll send the run sheet by Thursday. Have a great show. |

## Flattery

Answer the question. Don't praise it, or agree with the reader before you
answer.

| Before                                                              | After                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Great question! You're absolutely right that refunds can be tricky. | Refunds go back to the card you paid with within 5 business days. |

## The rules for every writer

A draft also breaks the rules on
[Language and grammar](/content/language-and-grammar). These catch the most.

- [Every line carries a fact](/content/language-and-grammar#every-line-carries-a-fact)
  catches vague claims and flourish.
- [Use the plain word](/content/language-and-grammar#use-the-plain-word) and
  [Say is and has](/content/language-and-grammar#say-is-and-has) catch
  inflated words.
- [Cut filler](/content/language-and-grammar#cut-filler) and
  [Hedge once](/content/language-and-grammar#hedge-once) catch padding.
- [Whole sentences](/content/language-and-grammar#whole-sentences) catches
  arrows and fragments.
- [Dashes](/content/language-and-grammar#dashes) bans em dashes and en dashes.
- [Name the source](/content/language-and-grammar#name-the-source) catches
  "experts say".
