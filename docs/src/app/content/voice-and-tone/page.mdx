Oztix is Australia's largest independent ticketing company, and has worked
with events and venues for over 20 years. Our voice stays the same everywhere:
in docs, interfaces, support, and marketing. Our tone shifts with the moment
and the reader.

## Personality traits

Three traits shape every line we write.

| Trait          | What it means                                                       | Write like                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Keep it real   | Skip corporate speak. Share honest advice from real experience.     | An experienced friend giving honest advice |
| Make it happen | Solve the problem directly, take ownership, and name the next step. | A capable partner focused on the fix       |
| In it together | Build for the long term and celebrate shared wins.                  | A team member invested in the outcome      |

| Write                                              | Not                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Here's what worked for venues your size last year. | Leverage our industry-leading insights. |
| I'll fix the seating map and call you by 3pm.      | Your request has been logged.           |
| Let's look at what this means for your next show.  | Thank you for your business.            |

## Principles

### Show, don't sell

Lead with real results, and back every claim with an example or a number.

| Write                                                                       | Not                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Here's how three Brisbane venues used these reports to lift their presales. | Our platform offers powerful analytics. |

### Keep momentum going

Remove the obstacle and give the next step, so the reader can act today.

| Write                                        | Not                                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Here are three things we can start on today. | We'll schedule a meeting to discuss options. |

### Share the journey

Celebrate the wins we share, and talk about where they lead next.

| Write                                                                                | Not                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Ochre Kite Weekender sold out a week sooner than last year. Let's plan the next one. | Thank you for your business. |

### Speak their language

Use the terms and examples of the reader's market.

| Write                                                            | Not                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| This works for your intimate gigs and your festival main stages. | Our solution scales effectively. |

### Own the outcome

Take responsibility and follow through until the problem stays fixed.

| Write                                            | Not                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| I'll sort this out and make sure it stays fixed. | We'll pass this to our technical team. |

## Tone for the moment

The voice stays the same, and the tone follows what the reader is going
through.

| Moment                   | Tone                                                                  | Example                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Things are going well    | Celebrate the shared win and name its impact, with humble confidence. | Your early-bird release paid off. 2,000 fans bought in the first hour. Let's look at what this means for your next event. |
| Something has gone wrong | Own it now, share what we know, and say when the next update comes.   | The scanning app is running slow. Here's what we know, what we're doing about it, and when we'll update you next.         |
| Something is new         | Lead with the practical benefit, a real example, and a timeline.      | We built this because you told us your festival entry lines were too slow. Here's how it works and when you can start.    |
| Giving support           | Act straight away, give a clear timeline, and follow through.         | I can see the issue with your seating map. I'm fixing it now and will call you in 10 minutes with an update.              |

## Markets

Each market has its own terms and measures. Use them, and pick examples from
the reader's market.

| Market     | Covers                                         | Lead with                                                 | Example                                                               |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Live music | Concerts, festivals, and tours                 | Artist and promoter terms, fan experience, venue capacity | We'll handle your whole calendar, from pub gigs to stadium shows.     |
| Venues     | Hospitality, performance, arenas, and outdoors | Operations terms, space use, crowd flow                   | Let's optimise your floor plan to lift both capacity and bar revenue. |
| Shows      | Agricultural, consumer, and spectator shows    | Show society terms, exhibitors and visitors, the seasons  | We'll manage your pavilion entries and your grand parade timing.      |
| Sports     | Motorsport, racing, rodeos, and sporting codes | Code terms, member access, game-day flow                  | We'll run your season passes and your match-day sales in one place.   |
| Events     | Tourism, lifestyle, and community              | Visitor experience, local impact, regional patterns       | We'll help turn your food festival into a must-visit regional event.  |

## Headlines, body copy, and calls to action

| Part           | Rule                                            | Write                              | Not                                  |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Headline       | Direct, outcome first, and active.              | Sell out your next show sooner     | Unlocking The Power Of Ticketing     |
| Body copy      | Plain English, short paragraphs, real examples. | Doors open at 7pm. Bring photo ID. | Patrons are advised to arrive early. |
| Call to action | An active verb that names the next step.        | Buy tickets                        | Click here                           |

## Guidelines

  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont example={<Button intent='accent' emphasis='strong'>Click here</Button>}>
    Don't make the reader guess what happens next.
  </Guideline.Dont>

<Guideline title='Own the problem'>
  <Guideline.Do
    example={
      <Callout intent='warning' title='Scanning is running slow'>
        We're fixing it now and will update you by 7:15pm.
      </Callout>
    }
  >
    Say what's wrong, what we're doing, and when the reader hears next.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont
    example={
      <Callout intent='warning' title='An issue has been detected'>
        Your request has been passed on.
      </Callout>
    }
  >
    Don't hide who is acting or leave the reader waiting with no time.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Before you publish

Ask these questions of every piece of writing.

- Does it sound like a person who knows the industry?
- Does it solve the reader's problem or name their next step?
- Do the examples come from the reader's market?
- Does it build a relationship that lasts past this sale?

## In the interface

- [Button](/components/button) labels name the action, such as Buy tickets.
- [EmptyState](/components/empty-state) says what happened and what to do next.
- [Callout](/components/callout) messages own a problem and give a time for
  the next update.
- [Field](/components/field) error text says how to fix the value, not only
  that it's wrong.
