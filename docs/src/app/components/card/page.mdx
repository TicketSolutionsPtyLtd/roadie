A container for grouping related content with optional header and footer.

## Import

```tsx
import { Card } from '@oztix/roadie-components/card'
```

## Examples

### Variants

`variant` sets the card's shape. `default` is a plain card and applies when you leave the prop off. `ticket` cuts a notch into each side where the body meets `Card.Footer`, with a perforated line between them. The notches are real holes, so whatever sits behind the card shows through. `ticket` works with every emphasis. On `subtler`, the fill clears when the card is interactive, the same as a plain subtler card.

### Direction

`direction` lays out the card's parts and works on any card. `vertical` stacks them and applies when you leave the prop off. `horizontal` gives `Card.Footer` a column of its own beside the rest of the card, sized to its contents. `auto` picks between the two: the card stacks in a narrow container and splits at 30rem and up, laying out exactly as `horizontal` does once it splits. An `auto` card is a container named `card`, so its children can use `@min-[30rem]/card:` and resolve against the card rather than a container further up.

Direction moves the footer, it does not change what belongs in each part. Keep the body in `Card.Content` and leave the footer for the stub, such as actions or counts.

The footer's column is as wide as its content, so cards in a list can have columns of different widths. Set `--card-footer-size` once on the list to line them up. Every side column is then at least that wide, and a ticket's perforation follows it. A stacked footer ignores it.

```tsx
<div className='grid gap-4 [--card-footer-size:11rem]'>
  {events.map((event) => (
    <Card key={event.id} variant='ticket' direction='auto'>…</Card>
  ))}
</div>
```

### With image

The subtler card has padding around the image, giving it inset rounded corners. The normal card uses a full-bleed image with `overflow-hidden`.

`Card.Image` is an [`Image`](/components/image). Pass `width` (and `height`) to serve a right-sized WebP instead of the original, `sizes` so the fluid card loads small files on small screens, and `placeholder='blur'` for a blur-up while it loads.


### Composition

Every card has a `Card.Content`, which holds its body. `Card.Header` above it and `Card.Footer` below it are optional. A header has no bottom padding, so a card with only a title and description puts them in `Card.Content`, as the [main link](#with-a-main-link) example does. A card without `Card.Content` warns in development. Keep a `Suspense` boundary inside `Card.Content`, not around it, or the card warns while it loads.

```tsx-live
<Card>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title>Quokka Thunder Weekender</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>3-day music festival, Jumbuck Orchard Park</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Content>
    <p>Three days of live music across four stages featuring local and international artists. Food trucks, art installations, and late-night DJ sets.</p>
  </Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer>
    <Button size='sm'>Buy tickets</Button>
    <Button size='sm' emphasis='subtler'>Learn more</Button>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card>
```

### A document

doesn't respond to hover, press, or focus. That makes it the surface for a
document, such as a tax invoice or a booking confirmation. Render it as an
`<article>`, and set `Card.Content`'s padding with `className`, which replaces
the default. This invoice is tighter on a phone and roomier from `sm`.

### With a main link

`href` on `Card` makes the whole card one link, so nothing else inside it can be a link or a button. When a card also holds other actions, wrap its title in `Card.Link` instead. The link covers the card, so a click anywhere follows it, and the other links and buttons sit above it and stay clickable. The card takes its hover, press and focus states from the link, and screen readers hear a short link named by the title rather than the whole card.

```tsx-live
<Card variant='ticket' direction='auto' emphasis='raised' className='max-w-lg'>
  <Card.Content>
    <Card.Title>
      <Card.Link href='#weekly-pass'>Weekly Pass</Card.Link>
    </Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>Mon 3 to Fri 7 May · Barnacle Bowl Amphitheatre, Rockhampton</Card.Description>
  </Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer>
    <Button size='sm' href='#tickets'>
      <TicketIcon weight='bold' className='size-4' />
      638 tickets
    </Button>
    <Button size='sm' href='#shared'>
      <UsersIcon weight='bold' className='size-4' />
      376 shared
    </Button>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card>
```
