A container for grouping related content with optional header and footer.

## Import

```tsx
import { Card } from '@oztix/roadie-components/card'
```

## Examples

### Default

```tsx-live
<Card href='#'>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title>Midnight Frequency</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>Live at The Anchorage, Brisbane</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Content>
    <p>An evening of electronic beats and ambient soundscapes under the stars.</p>
  </Card.Content>
</Card>
```

### Variants

`variant` sets the card's shape. `default` is a plain card and applies when you leave the prop off. `ticket` cuts a notch into each side where the body meets `Card.Footer`, with a perforated line between them. The notches are real holes, so whatever sits behind the card shows through. `ticket` works with every emphasis. On `subtler`, the fill clears when the card is interactive, the same as a plain subtler card.

```tsx-live
<div className='grid max-w-sm gap-6'>
```

### Direction

`direction` lays out the card's parts and works on any card. `vertical` stacks them and applies when you leave the prop off. `horizontal` gives `Card.Footer` a column of its own beside the rest of the card, sized to its contents. `auto` picks between the two: the card stacks in a narrow container and splits at 30rem and up, laying out exactly as `horizontal` does once it splits.

Direction moves the footer, it does not change what belongs in each part. Keep the body in `Card.Content` and leave the footer for the stub, such as actions or counts.

### With image

The subtler card has padding around the image, giving it inset rounded corners. The normal card uses a full-bleed image with `overflow-hidden`.

`Card.Image` is an [`Image`](/components/image). Pass `width` (and `height`) to serve a right-sized WebP instead of the original, `sizes` so the fluid card loads small files on small screens, and `placeholder='blur'` for a blur-up while it loads.

```tsx-live
<div className='grid grid-cols-2 gap-4'>
  <Card href='#' emphasis='subtler'>
    <Card.Image
      src='https://assets.oztix.com.au/image/1226ab55-3d53-47f0-ab4c-cddcf02bb001.png'
      alt='Sunset Harbour Cruise'
      className='aspect-square'
      width={300}
      height={300}
      sizes='(min-width: 640px) 25vw, 50vw'
      placeholder='blur'
    />
    <Card.Header>
      <Card.Title>Sunset Harbour Cruise</Card.Title>
      <Card.Description>
        <DateTime
          at={new Date('2026-05-17T08:00:00Z')}
          timeZone='Australia/Brisbane'
          timeStyle='medium'
        />
      </Card.Description>
      <Card.Description>Riverside Wharf</Card.Description>
    </Card.Header>
    <Card.Footer>
      <p className='text-strong font-semibold'>$89</p>
    </Card.Footer>
  </Card>
  <Card href='#' className='overflow-hidden'>
    <Card.Image
      src='https://assets.oztix.com.au/image/1226ab55-3d53-47f0-ab4c-cddcf02bb001.png'
      alt='Long Table Dinner'
      width={600}
      height={300}
      sizes='(min-width: 640px) 25vw, 50vw'
    />
    <Card.Header>
      <Card.Description>Dining &amp; Social</Card.Description>
      <Card.Title>Long Table Dinner with Marco Bellucci</Card.Title>
      <Card.Description>
        <DateTime at={new Date('2026-05-24T08:00:00Z')} timeZone='Australia/Brisbane' />{' '}
        &middot; <Duration of='PT3H' durationStyle='medium' />
      </Card.Description>
    </Card.Header>
  </Card>
</div>
```

### Composition

Use `Card.Header`, `Card.Content`, and `Card.Footer` to structure content.

```tsx-live
<Card>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title>Sunset Sounds Festival</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>3-day music festival, Bayshore Parklands</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Content>
    <p>Three days of live music across four stages featuring local and international artists. Food trucks, art installations, and late-night DJ sets.</p>
  </Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer>
    <Button size='sm'>Buy tickets</Button>
    <Button size='sm' emphasis='subtler'>Learn more</Button>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card>
```
