The Oztix logo, in fixed brand colours that never follow the theme.

## Import

```tsx
import { Logo } from '@oztix/roadie-components/logo'
```

## Examples

### Default

```tsx-live
<Logo />
```

### Variants

`normal` is the mark and the wordmark together. `mark` is the mark alone, for tight spaces such as an app icon or a collapsed navigation. `wordmark` is the word alone, as a collapse state of the full logo, never as a logo of its own.

```tsx-live
<div className='grid justify-items-start gap-6'>
  <Logo variant='normal' />
  <Logo variant='mark' />
  <Logo variant='wordmark' />
</div>
```

### Product

`product` pairs the mark with a product name, rendered as live text in place of the wordmark. When it's set, `variant` is ignored.

```tsx-live
<Logo product='Studio' />
```

### Tagline

`tagline` adds the tagline, Built on Partnerships, after a divider. Use it wherever there's room. A `mark`, `wordmark`, or `product` logo takes no tagline.

The tagline keeps its title case, an approved exception to sentence case.

```tsx-live
<Logo tagline />
```

### Layout

`layout='stacked'` sets the wordmark under the mark, with the tagline under that when it's on. Use it for square or narrow spaces, such as a social profile. A product logo stays horizontal.

```tsx-live layout=row gap=8
<Logo layout='stacked' />
<Logo layout='stacked' tagline />
```

### Sizes

The logo's height is set with the `size` prop, not a className. One value scales the mark, the gap, the tagline, and the product name together. `xs` is 20px, `sm` 24px, `md` 32px (the default), `lg` 40px, `xl` 48px. A stacked logo sizes its mark, and is 1.5 times the mark wide.

`xs` is for product UI only, such as a dense app header. Everywhere else, a logo is at least 24px high, and a stacked one at least 48px wide, which is `md`.

Logo sizes sit one step below Button and IconTile, whose `md` is 40px, so a `size='md'` logo pairs with a `size='sm'` button.

```tsx-live
<div className='grid justify-items-start gap-6'>
  <Logo size='xs' />
  <Logo size='sm' />
  <Logo size='md' />
  <Logo size='lg' />
  <Logo size='xl' />
  <Logo product='Studio' size='lg' />
</div>
```

### Tone

`tone` picks the artwork's colour, which stays the same in light and dark mode and ignores a surrounding intent. `brand`, the default, is Oztix blue. `dark` is one dark colour, for a light background where blue lacks contrast or the logo sits among other neutral logos. `light` is one light colour, for dark, brand, or photo backgrounds.

The brand tagline and divider follow the theme, so they stay readable in both modes. The `dark` and `light` taglines are fixed like the rest of the artwork.

```tsx-live layout=stack gap=4
{/* Brand */}
<Logo tone='brand' tagline />
{/* Dark */}
<div className='rounded-lg bg-(--color-neutral-light-0) p-4'>
  <Logo tone='dark' tagline />
</div>
{/* Light */}
<div className='intent-brand emphasis-strong rounded-lg p-4'>
  <Logo tone='light' tagline />
</div>
```

### Collapsing

The `logo-wordmark` and `logo-product` slots hold their own gap and clip, so a parent can collapse either to the mark's square by animating its `grid-template-columns` from `1fr` to `0fr`.

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Size the logo with the size prop'>
  <Guideline.Do code={`<Logo size='lg' />`}>
    Set one height through <code>size</code>. The width follows the logo's proportions.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Logo className='h-12 w-40' />`}>
    Don't set a width or a separate height. That stretches or crops the artwork and leaves the gap and product name at the old size.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep product names short'>
  <Guideline.Do example={<Logo product='Studio' />}>
    Use the product's one- or two-word name, in title case.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont example={<Logo product='Oztix Studio' />}>
    Don't repeat "Oztix" or add a tagline. The mark already says Oztix, and the name never wraps.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Pick a tone for the background'>
  <Guideline.Do code={`<Logo tone='light' />`}>
    Set <code>tone</code> to suit what's behind the logo. The logo doesn't take an intent and doesn't follow one around it.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Logo className='text-[#0191eb]' />`}>
    Don't hard-code a colour. Only the three tones are approved artwork.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep the wordmark with the mark'>
  <Guideline.Do code={`<Logo />`}>
    Show the mark and the wordmark together. The wordmark alone is only a collapse state, while a navigation folds the full logo.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Logo variant='wordmark' />`}>
    Don't place the wordmark as a logo on its own.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Keep 20px logos in product UI'>
  <Guideline.Do code={`<Logo size='sm' tagline />`}>
    Use 24px or larger in marketing, print, and partner material.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Logo size='xs' tagline />`}>
    Don't use the 20px logo outside product UI.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Accessibility

- The logo is one image with the accessible name "Oztix". A product logo is named "Oztix" plus the product, such as "Oztix Studio", and a logo with the tagline is named "Oztix, Built on Partnerships".
- Pass `aria-label` to name it something else, such as "Oztix home" inside a link.
- When the logo sits next to text that already says Oztix, pass `aria-hidden` and it drops its role and name.
- The inner SVGs are always hidden from assistive technology.
- The logo is always left to right, so a right-to-left page never mirrors the lockup. It takes no `dir` prop.
