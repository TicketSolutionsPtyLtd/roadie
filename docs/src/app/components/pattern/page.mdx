The Oztix swirl pattern, cropped to fill a banner, panel, or strip in a fixed brand colour.

## Import

```tsx
import { Pattern } from '@oztix/roadie-components/pattern'
```

## Examples

### Default

The pattern fills its parent in both axes and never sizes itself, so give the parent a size. It has no radius of its own: it usually sits on an edge, so the container rounds and clips it, here with `rounded-3xl overflow-hidden`. The default is the `right` crop in brand blue.

```tsx-live
<div className='h-64 w-40 overflow-hidden rounded-3xl'>
  <Pattern />
</div>
```

### Crops

`crop` says where the pattern sits. Like a `background-size: cover` image, the whole artwork scales with the box until it covers it, so a small strip shows the same swirls as a hero, only smaller. The crop then anchors the artwork, like `background-position`, and cuts off the rest.

#### Banner

`banner` is for a wide hero, with the artwork centred. Set content on a raised surface over it, never straight on the swirls.

```tsx-live
<section className='relative grid min-h-64 place-items-center overflow-hidden rounded-3xl p-6'>
  <Pattern crop='banner' className='absolute inset-0' />
  <div className='relative grid max-w-sm justify-items-start gap-3 rounded-2xl emphasis-raised p-6'>
    <h2 className='text-display-ui-3 text-strong'>Summer at Nimbus Thistle Hall</h2>
    <p className='text-subtle'>12 nights of live music from 3 January.</p>
    <Button intent='brand' emphasis='strong'>See the line-up</Button>
  </div>
</section>
```

#### Left and right

`left` and `right` are for a panel on that side of the content, using a portrait arrangement of the artwork anchored to that edge.

```tsx-live
<div className='grid grid-cols-[2fr_3fr] overflow-hidden rounded-3xl emphasis-raised'>
  <Pattern crop='left' />
  <div className='grid content-center gap-2 p-6'>
    <h2 className='text-display-ui-4 text-strong'>Sell your first show</h2>
    <p className='text-subtle'>Set up your event, ticket types, and payouts in one place.</p>
  </div>
</div>
```

#### Top and bottom

`top` and `bottom` are for a strip along that edge of a card or page, anchored to that edge. The card clips its own corners with `overflow-hidden`, so the strip takes its radius from the card.

```tsx-live width=80
<Card emphasis='raised' className='overflow-hidden'>
  <div className='h-16'>
    <Pattern crop='top' tone='brand-purple' />
  </div>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title>Marzipan Thunderclap</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>Fri 12 Mar · Nimbus Thistle Hall, Newcastle</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <div className='h-6'>
    <Pattern crop='bottom' tone='brand-purple' />
  </div>
</Card>
```

### Tones

`tone` picks the brand colour: `brand-blue` (the default) for every market, `brand-purple` for music and venues, and `brand-orange` for shows, events, and sports. Each draws step 5 swirls on a step 9 ground of the `brand`, `info`, or `brand-secondary` scale. Like all brand artwork, the colour stays the same in light and dark mode and ignores a surrounding intent.

```tsx-live layout=row gap=4
<div className='h-48 w-32 overflow-hidden rounded-2xl'>
  <Pattern tone='brand-blue' />
</div>
<div className='h-48 w-32 overflow-hidden rounded-2xl'>
  <Pattern tone='brand-purple' />
</div>
<div className='h-48 w-32 overflow-hidden rounded-2xl'>
  <Pattern tone='brand-orange' />
</div>
```

### Behind the logo

The pattern frames a branded header or footer with the logo on a strip across it. Let the pattern bleed off the edges.

```tsx-live
<div className='relative grid h-40 items-center overflow-hidden rounded-3xl'>
  <Pattern crop='banner' className='absolute inset-0' />
  <div className='relative grid justify-items-center bg-(--color-neutral-light-0) py-4'>
    <Logo size='lg' />
  </div>
</div>
```

## Guidelines

<Guidelines>

<Guideline title='Keep text off the swirls'>
  <Guideline.Do
    code={`
      <div className='relative grid place-items-center'>
        <Pattern crop='banner' className='absolute inset-0' />
        <div className='relative rounded-2xl emphasis-raised p-6'>…</div>
      </div>`}
  >
    Set headings and body text on a raised surface over the pattern, or beside it.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont
    code={`
      <div className='relative'>
        <Pattern className='absolute inset-0' />
        <p className='relative'>Doors 7pm. Bring your ticket on your phone.</p>
      </div>`}
  >
    Don't set text straight on the pattern. The swirls cross every line, and no text colour reads on both the ground and the lines.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Use the three tones'>
  <Guideline.Do code={`<Pattern tone='brand-orange' />`}>
    Pick blue, purple, or orange with <code>tone</code>.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Pattern className='opacity-50 hue-rotate-90' />`}>
    Don't recolour, fade, or filter the pattern. Only the three tones are approved artwork.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

<Guideline title='Size the parent, not the pattern'>
  <Guideline.Do
    code={`
      <div className='h-48'>
        <Pattern crop='right' />
      </div>`}
  >
    Give the parent a size and pick the crop for where the pattern sits. The crop fills it without stretching.
  </Guideline.Do>
  <Guideline.Dont code={`<Pattern className='h-48 w-96 rotate-90 -scale-x-100' />`}>
    Don't size, rotate, or flip the pattern itself. Choose another crop instead.
  </Guideline.Dont>
</Guideline>

</Guidelines>

## Accessibility

- The pattern is decoration. It's always hidden from assistive technology and can't take focus, so it takes no label.
- Anything the pattern stands for, such as the market it's coloured for, is said in text too.
