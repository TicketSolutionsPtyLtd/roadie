# 0005 Roadie mostly wins over brand

## Context

The brand guidelines and Roadie disagree in places, such as greys, yellow,
contrast, and formats. Two sources of truth would drift.

## Decision

Where they disagree, Roadie mostly wins and the brand republishes from it.
Roadie's warning yellow stays darker than the brand's for contrast, and the
brand adopts APCA Lc 60 for labels on strong fills. Exceptions are named one
at a time, such as the logo tagline's title case.

## Consequences

Brand and Content move into the Roadie docs as the source. Brand assets use
Roadie tokens, so they follow token changes.
