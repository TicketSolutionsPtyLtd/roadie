# 0006 Writing rules

## Context

Docs and copy were written to no shared standard, and agent prose drifted
into dashes, filler, and title case.

## Decision

We adopt unslop (Lauren Tan, MIT) in Oztix wording: the Oxford comma, no em
or en dashes, no colons in headings, and parentheses kept for prices and
abbreviations.

## Consequences

Text is easy to scan and the same across docs, UI, and brand. Reviewers flag
breaches like any other convention.
