Add `Switch`, an on or off control for settings that apply straight away, built
on Base UI's switch primitive. Pass `label` and `description` for a labelled
settings row, or drop it into `Field` and it takes its name, helper or error
text, and `invalid`, `required` and `disabled` from there. The checked track is
accent unless you pass an `intent`, the thumb slides with a transition that
stops under reduced motion, and `size` is `sm` or `md`.
