Add `Switch`, an on or off control for settings that apply straight away. Pass
`label` and `description` for a labelled settings row, or drop it into `Field`
and it takes its name, helper or error text, and `invalid`, `required` and
`disabled` from there. The checked track is accent, the thumb slides with a
transition that stops under reduced motion, and `size` is `sm` or `md`.
