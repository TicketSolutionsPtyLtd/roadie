Read an HTML email's buttons and columns on import. A link alone on its line becomes a button when it's padded or `inline-block`, or fills a padded cell, keeping its centre or right alignment, and a row of two to four cells whose widths add up to 100% becomes columns, where both used to become plain text. The import dialog also opens an `.html` or `.mjml` file with Choose a file.
