Add block selection to editors with a block handle. `Escape` selects the caret's block, and `Shift+ArrowUp` and `Shift+ArrowDown` take in the blocks above or below. With several blocks selected, by these keys or by a text selection across them, Move up, Move down, Duplicate, Delete, Turn into, and their keys act on them all, and the block menu is headed with how many, such as "3 blocks". Selected blocks take one accent ring, rounded as a section's, the same as a selected image or custom block (which had none before), and the floating toolbar hides. One selected block keeps its own menu, headed by its type. Undo after deleting selected blocks brings them back selected. Typing or `Enter` carries on at the end of the selection, rather than replacing it. A second `Escape` goes on to whatever is around the editor, such as a dialog. Move up and Move down keep the menu on a section as it moves. Typing over a selection that runs out of a section no longer comes out reversed in a new section. A block dragged by its handle now lands before the block under the pointer, or after it once the pointer is past its middle.
