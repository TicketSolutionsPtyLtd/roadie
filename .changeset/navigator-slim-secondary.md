Update `Navigator`'s generated list pane to take less room. It stops at 18rem beside the page instead of 24rem, with 40px rows (44px on touch screens), smaller titles, no dividers, and a narrower inset. A `searchable` list now starts with a search button in its header, opened by ⌘/ or Ctrl+/, and its field is a plain 40px field rather than a raised 48px one.
