The email preset's ready-made sections lead the Blocks drawer and are in the `/` menu too, found by name or as `/lineup` and `/unsubscribe`. A ready-made section now goes after the top-level block with the caret, or before an empty top-level line, so never inside another section, and its first line's text is selected, so typing replaces the placeholder. Any block the drawer or `/` adds selects its first line's text the same way.
