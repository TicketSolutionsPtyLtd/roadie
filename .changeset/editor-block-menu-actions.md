Add Turn into, Insert below, and Copy link to block to the block menu. Turn into makes a text block a paragraph, a heading, a list, or a quote, keeping its `blockId`; Insert below opens a line under the block with the insert menu; and Copy link to block copies the page's address with the block's `blockId` as its hash, which scrolls the editor to the block when opened, and says Link copied by the grip. Move up and Move down now leave the menu open on the moved block. The menu opens beside the grip, or below it where there's no room, and `Mod+/` opens it with its first action ready. On a phone the grip has a 44px target, and the text is inset at its start by the grip's room, in a `subtler` editor too, with no add button beside it. The menu opens as a half-height sheet below its block, led by Move up, Move down and a strong Done, with no scrim, as Settings has, with Turn into and Move to as steps of their own and Delete last. Move to puts the block under one of the page's headings or sections. A `blocks` editor makes room for its grip at its start on a desktop too, in a `subtler` editor as well, so neither its host nor a frame clips it. The grip lines up with a section's first line of text rather than its padding, follows the editor as it resizes, and on a phone follows the caret onto a new line.
