Add Turn into, Insert below, and Copy link to block to the block menu. Turn into makes a text block a paragraph, a heading, a list, or a quote, keeping its `blockId`; Insert below opens a line under the block with the insert menu; and Copy link to block copies the page's address with the block's `blockId` as its hash, which scrolls the editor to the block when opened. Move up and Move down now leave the menu open on the moved block. On a phone the grip has a 44px target and its menu opens as a sheet from the bottom, and the text sits as far from each edge, with no add button beside it.
