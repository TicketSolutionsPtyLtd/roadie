Add Turn into, Insert below, and Copy link to block to the block menu. Turn into makes a text block a paragraph, a heading, a list, or a quote, keeping its `blockId`; Insert below opens a line under the block with the insert menu; and Copy link to block copies the page's address with the block's `blockId` as its hash, which scrolls the editor to the block when opened, and says Link copied by the grip. Move up and Move down now leave the menu open on the moved block. On a phone the grip has a 44px target, and the text sits as far from each edge, in a `subtler` editor too, with no add button beside it. The menu opens as a half-height sheet below its block, led by Move up, Move down and Done, with Turn into as a step of its own and Delete last.
