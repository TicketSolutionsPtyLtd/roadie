`DataCard` and `Chart` take `controls`: switches for what the card's body shows, such as a chart's Cumulative, Incremental and Percentage modes. They sit in a full-width row under the headline, wrap on a narrow card, and stay in place in every state and in a chart's Table view. `StatTile` doesn't take them.
