Add `StackedArea` (`@oztix/roadie-charts/stacked-area`), a chart for part of a whole over time, such as scans by gate. The largest layer stacks at the bottom, past 6 the smallest roll into Other on top, and the tooltip opens with the total. Dashboards take it as plot kind `stacked-area`, with `/static` and `/tables` support.
