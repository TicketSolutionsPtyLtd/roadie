Add `Editor.BubbleMenu`, the floating toolbar over selected text for `toolbar='floating'`. Alt+F10 moves focus to it, opening it at the caret when nothing is selected. It hides when the selection collapses, on a selected image or embed, on text that takes no marks, while the link editor is open, and when the editor is read-only or disabled.
