Add `Editor.BubbleMenu`, a menu of Bold, Italic, Underline and Link over selected text. It's opt-in: add `<Editor.BubbleMenu />` among the editor's parts. It hides on a collapsed selection, a selected image or embed, while the link editor is open, and when the editor is read-only or disabled, and Alt+F10 moves focus to it while it shows.
