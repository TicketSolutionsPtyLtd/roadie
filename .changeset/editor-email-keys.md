In an email's columns, Tab moves the caret to the start of the next column and Shift+Tab to the end of the one before, then out of the row, as Tab moves between a table's cells, so what's typed next lands there rather than focus leaving the editor. A list item in a column that can indent or outdent still does. Enter on a selected merge tag opens its form rather than splitting the line.
