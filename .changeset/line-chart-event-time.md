Add an `eventTime` x format and `EVENT_DAY_START_HOUR` to LineChart and the dashboard `line` plot, for comparing events on their own clock. `x` is hours from midnight starting the first event day, and a day runs to 6am. Ticks read "Day 2" and "19:00", and tooltips, tables and screen readers say "Day 2, 7:15pm".
