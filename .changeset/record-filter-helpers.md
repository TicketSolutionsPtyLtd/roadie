Add `parseRecordValue`, `recordFilterKey`, and `recordFilterFits` to `@oztix/roadie-core/records`, for an app that builds its own filter or search UI. `parseRecordValue` reads text as one field's value, as `field:value` does; `recordFilterKey` is a filter's identity, as `equalRecordViews` compares chips; `recordFilterFits` says whether your fields can apply a filter.
