The Link, Edit link, and Alt text popovers apply what's typed as it's typed, as Button settings and Merge tag do: a selection's link once it's a whole address, an embed's player once a provider reads its link, and an image's alt text at once. Enter, the check, or a click away keeps the changes as one undo step, and Escape or Close puts them back. Adding a link at a caret, or a new embed, still waits for save.
