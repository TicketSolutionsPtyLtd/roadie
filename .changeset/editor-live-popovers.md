The Link and Edit link popovers apply a selection's link as it's typed, while it's a whole address, as Button settings and Merge tag do. Enter, the check, Escape, or a click away keeps the changes as one undo step, and Close puts them back. Embed settings changes an embed's player while a provider reads its link, and Image settings an image's alt text at once. Adding a link at a caret, or a new embed, still waits for save.
