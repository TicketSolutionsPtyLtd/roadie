Keep the editor's docked toolbar on one row at any width. The controls that don't fit go into a More menu at its end, after Undo, rather than wrapping onto a second row or scrolling sideways out of sight on a phone. Blocks and the block type always stay, so a phone's bar leads with them. On a touch screen, while you edit, the bar sits at the bottom of the screen above the keyboard, and the bar holds still while More is open. It stays there while you use the toolbar's menus or the editor's sheets and settings. On a phone, the bar leaves out the text controls while a block is selected.
