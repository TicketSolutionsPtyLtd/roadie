Let a block's `illustration` take any spot illustration in `@oztix/roadie-illustrations`, such as `Number1` or `LetterB`, not only the 12 it offered. Build a CMS's picker from `@oztix/roadie-illustrations/spot/catalogue`. Cards `steps` now numbers steps 1 to 9 with the number illustrations, and numbers a step from 10 in text, as before. Those illustrations keep their own colours, so an intent no longer colours a step's number below 10. Steps now share one width, up to 24rem, with as many in a row as fit at 15rem or more, 1.5rem apart. A short row, or a short last row, is centred under a centred heading rather than leaving an empty column.
