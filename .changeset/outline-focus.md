Add `outline-focus` and `outline-focus-danger`, which colour an outline as the focus ring: accent step 9, or danger step 9, at `--focus-ring-opacity`. Use them on a control that widens its own outline on focus, in place of an arbitrary `outline-[color-mix(…)]` class. A later one replaces an earlier one in `cn()`.
