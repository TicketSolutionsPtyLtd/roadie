An email button has settings: in its toolbar, Button settings edits its link, its style (filled or outline), its width (its label's or its column's), and its alignment (left, centre or right). The editor and `renderEmail` both draw them. The button saves only what isn't the default, as `align`, `emphasis: 'normal'` and `fullWidth: true`. The toolbar's Edit link is now Button settings.
