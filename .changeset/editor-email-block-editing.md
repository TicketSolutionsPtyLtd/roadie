Add buttons, column counts, and spacer heights to the `email` preset's editing. Type `/` and choose Button to add one, with a link that must be https, http, mailto, or tel, and its label selected to type over. Enter in a button starts a new paragraph after it. A button's toolbar opens Button settings or removes the button, columns get a menu of 2, 3, or 4 (a removed column's blocks move into the last column left), and a selected spacer gets a Height menu. In columns, Tab moves the caret to the start of the next column and Shift+Tab to the end of the one before, then out of the row (Tab opens a line after a row that ends the email or its section), as Tab moves between a table's cells, so what's typed next lands there rather than focus leaving the editor. A list item in a column that can indent or outdent still does. The columns' toolbar takes the first place that covers no text: past the columns' end, above their end or start, in their top-end or top-start corner, in the top padding of the section holding them, or in a clear gap between their lines, and never below them over the next block's controls. The spacer's toolbar sits past the spacer's end where the frame has room, or inside it on a narrow screen.
