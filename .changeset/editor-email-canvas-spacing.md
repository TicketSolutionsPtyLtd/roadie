The email canvas spaces its blocks as the email does: 16px between blocks, 16px above and below a section, and 24px between side-by-side columns, with headings at the email's sizes. A section or a column no longer pads or borders its blocks; it shows a dashed outline, outside its box, under the pointer or while the caret is in it.
