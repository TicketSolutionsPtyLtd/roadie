Keep the editor's block toolbars and Button settings clear of what's around them. Button settings opens under the button rather than over it, as a drawer from the bottom below 480px, and with focus on Emphasis, or on Link while the button has no link yet. The Columns and Spacer toolbars sit past their block's end where the frame has room, or inside its end where it hasn't, so neither covers the block before or after. An image's or embed's toolbar stays under the docked toolbar as the image scrolls under it, rather than drawing over it.
