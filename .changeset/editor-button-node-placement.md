Keep the editor's block toolbars and Button settings clear of what's around them. Button settings opens under the button rather than over it, as a drawer from the bottom below 480px, and with focus on Emphasis, or on Link while the button has no link yet. The Columns toolbar sits outside the columns, past their end where there's room, else above or below them, clear of the text around them, so it never covers their own text. The Spacer toolbar sits past the spacer's end where the frame has room, or inside it on a narrow screen. An image's or embed's toolbar stays under the docked toolbar as the image scrolls under it, rather than drawing over it.
