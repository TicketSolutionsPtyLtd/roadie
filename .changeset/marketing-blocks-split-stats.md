Add `Split` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/split`) and `Stats` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/stats`), blocks that set text beside an image, a partner lockup, or key numbers, and that show key numbers on their own.

`Split` has four presets: `feature-row`, an image beside a heading on a tinted panel; `media-text`, a plain image beside the text; `text-and-stats`, long-form text with a stats aside that wraps below on narrow screens; and `partner-spotlight`, which opens a partner page with the Oztix logo and the partner's logo joined by a heart. `Stats` has one, `figures`: a heading over a wrapping grid of figures, such as company facts, results, or a local audience, each as written, with a label and an optional spot illustration.
