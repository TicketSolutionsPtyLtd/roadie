Add `Split` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/split`) and `Stats` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/stats`), blocks that set text beside media, and that show key numbers on their own.

`Split` has two presets: `media-text`, the text beside an `image` or, with `stats`, long-form text beside key figures that wrap below on narrow screens; and `alternating-rows`, rows of a marked title and text beside a square image, alternating sides. `Stats` has one, `figures`: a heading over a wrapping grid of figures, each as written, with a label and an optional spot illustration.
