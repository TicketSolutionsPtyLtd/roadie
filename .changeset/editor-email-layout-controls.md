Add Centre to the email preset's text toolbar, for a heading or a paragraph, and Layout (Equal, Wide left 2:1, Wide right 1:2) and Stack on phone (Left first, Right first) to the columns toolbar. A column has a grip of its own, whose menu, also reached from the block menu's Column actions, moves the whole column left or right, duplicates it, or deletes it. A block's grip in a column now sits in the gap before its column, not over the column to its left, with no add button there; Insert below in its menu adds a block, on an empty line in place of it. On a phone or a touch screen the columns toolbar's three menus are one Layout menu, with a group for each, so it fits beside the columns. A spacer shows its dashed outline only under the pointer or at the caret, as a section does.
