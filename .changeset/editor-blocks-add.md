The blocks preset has the add button before each block's grip and Blocks in its toolbar, as the email preset does. The add button opens the `/` menu on a new line after the block, and Blocks opens a drawer of the preset's blocks as tiles. A `subtler` blocks editor hangs both in the page's gutter, so give it room at the start; a framed one makes room inside. The Blocks drawer, in both presets, gains a Find a block field that filters the tiles as `/` does, arrow-key movement between tiles with one Tab stop, and from 1280px wide it no longer blocks or dims the page, so the caret can move while it's open. Sections, such as an email's, lead the drawer. While it's open beside the page, the editor pads its end by as much as the drawer covers.
