The blocks preset has the add button before each block's grip and Blocks in its toolbar, as the email preset does. The add button opens the `/` menu on a new line after the block, and Blocks opens a drawer of the preset's blocks as tiles. A `subtler` blocks editor hangs both in the page's gutter, so give it room at the start; a framed one makes room inside.
