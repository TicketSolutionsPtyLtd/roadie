The tokens in `roadie.manifest.json` now include Tailwind's spacing unit (`--spacing`), its breakpoints (`--breakpoint-sm` to `--breakpoint-2xl`), and its container widths (`--container-3xs` to `--container-7xl`), beside Roadie's `--container-8xl`. Every `container-*` width the utility accepts is now listed.
