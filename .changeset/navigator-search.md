Add `Navigator.Search` to Navigator, a pinned Search item that opens the page's `CommandPalette` and shows its shortcut. It sits under the brand on a large screen and takes the phone bar's circle ahead of other pinned items, which fold into More. With `placement='automatic'` it ranks like any item, and with no palette on the page it renders nothing.
