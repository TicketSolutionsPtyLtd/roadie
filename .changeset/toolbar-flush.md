Add `flush` to `Toolbar`, for a bar docked along an edge. A flush toolbar drops its border, shadow, and pill shape, and `emphasis` sets only its fill. Its controls keep the inset every toolbar has, so a hover or focus ring never meets the edge. Draw a divider with `className`, such as `border-b border-subtler`, and clip the parent's corners with `overflow-clip`.
