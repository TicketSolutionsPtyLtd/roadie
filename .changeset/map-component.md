Add `Map` (`@oztix/roadie-components/map`), a map of places, such as where a venue is. It draws with MapLibre and OpenFreeMap tiles, loaded when a map first mounts, so it needs no API key or setup, and scrolling the page never zooms it. The map draws in Roadie's colours from the intent around it, following dark mode, or with `tone='natural'` in green parks, blue water and yellow main roads. `Map.Marker` pins places with an optional hover or tap preview, `Map.Picker` drops and drags a pin to choose a place, and flies the map to a `value` set from outside it, such as a form, `Map.Area` highlights a polygon with a click or tap preview, and `Map.Toolbar` adds joined zoom buttons, plus my-location and full screen buttons on their own. My location follows the reader with a pulsing dot until pressed again. `Map.Search` finds places from an app's own `suggest`, such as AddressFinder, on Roadie's `Autocomplete`, and flies the map to the one chosen; it exports the `Place` type. A marker or picker's `pin` swaps Roadie's pin for a graphic of the app's own, such as a spot illustration. With `expand`, the map fills the window and goes back without reloading, and `expanded`, `defaultExpanded` and `onExpandedChange` let an app's own button do the same. Engines can implement the optional `setCooperativeGestures`, so one finger pans a full screen map. `MapProvider` picks another engine or tiles for an app, and `googleEngine` (`@oztix/roadie-components/map-google`) draws with Google Maps for apps that show Google's search results.
