Add `ImageCrop` (`@oztix/roadie-components/image-crop`), for cropping an image to a ratio. The image pans, zooms and turns under a fixed frame: drag it, pinch or double-tap it on a touch screen or trackpad, use the arrow keys, `+`, `-` and `R`, or scroll once the crop area has focus. With no children it renders `ImageCrop.Stage` and `ImageCrop.Controls`, a floating pill with `ImageCrop.Zoom`, `ImageCrop.Rotate` (90° left) and `ImageCrop.Reset`. It always covers the frame: past an edge or a zoom limit the image gives way, then springs back. `minWidth` and `minHeight` stop zooming before the crop drops below them, and the size chip warns at that limit. `shape='circle'` masks the frame as a circle. `onValueChange` reports an `ImageCropValue` once the image loads, unless a given value already fits, and once each gesture, key press or button press ends, with the crop's `area` in the image's own pixels and as a `percent`. A polite live region says the crop once it settles.
