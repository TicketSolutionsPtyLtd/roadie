Add `Map` (`@oztix/roadie-components/map`), a map of places, such as where a venue is. It draws with MapLibre and OpenFreeMap tiles, loaded when a map first mounts, so it needs no API key or setup, and scrolling the page never zooms it. The map draws in Roadie's colours from the intent around it, following dark mode, or with `tone='natural'` in green parks, blue water and yellow main roads. `center`, `zoom` and `onViewChange` move the map from the app and say where it settles, and `actionsRef` takes `flyTo` and `fitTo`. `Map.Toolbar` adds joined zoom buttons, plus my-location and full screen buttons on their own. My location follows the reader with a pulsing dot until pressed again. With `expand`, the map fills the window and goes back without reloading, and `expanded`, `defaultExpanded` and `onExpandedChange` let an app's own button do the same. Engines can implement the optional `setCooperativeGestures`, so one finger pans a full screen map. MapLibre draws in a worker loaded from a file of its own, which Next.js, webpack and Vite builds serve with no setup; in Vite's dev server or another bundler, pass its address as `maplibreEngine({ workerUrl })`. `MapProvider` picks another engine or tiles for an app.
