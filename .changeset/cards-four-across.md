Cards' Text blocks and Steps presets set four cards across from a 76rem (1216px) block, down from 80rem, so a 1280px window with a classic, always-visible scrollbar keeps four in a row instead of dropping to two or three. Each card stays at least 15rem wide, except on a contained surface from 1216 to 1231px, where the panel's padding leaves them just under, down to 14.75rem.
