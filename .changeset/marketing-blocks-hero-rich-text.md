Add `Hero` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/hero`) and `RichText` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/rich-text`), the blocks that open a page and carry its text. Hero's `bento` preset sets the page's `h1` over a photo beside a looping video and two panels of cycling photos, `image` sets it beside one photo, and `title` is an eyebrow and a large title. RichText's `statement` preset centres a title and a few large paragraphs under an illustration, and `long-form` sets rich text in `Prose` at a reading width. Both take actions after their text.
