Add `Map.Clusters` to show places with values, such as ticket sales by postcode, as bands that add up the places near each other and split as the map zooms in. Each band shows its value and, below it, its share of the total. `Map.Toolbar`'s `lock` adds Lock groups, which keeps the groups as they are while the map zooms. `highlight` lights the bands holding places by label, such as while a row of a table beside the map is hovered. Show all fits the map to the data too.
