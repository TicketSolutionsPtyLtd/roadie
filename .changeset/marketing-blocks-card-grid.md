Add `CardGrid` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/card-grid`), a block of like items, such as benefits, services, values, or people, as cards in columns or alternating rows. Its presets are `features`, `services`, `industries`, `values`, `team`, and `lists`, and its columns follow the block's own width rather than the window's.
