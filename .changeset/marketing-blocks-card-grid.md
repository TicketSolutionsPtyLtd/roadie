Add `CardGrid` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/card-grid`), a block of like items, such as benefits, services, steps, contact options, values, or people, as cards in columns or alternating rows. Its presets are `features`, `services`, `industries`, `steps`, `links`, `alternating-rows`, `portraits`, and `lists`, and its columns follow the block's own width rather than the window's. An item with an `href` links its title and makes the whole card clickable, and `actions` adds links under the cards.
