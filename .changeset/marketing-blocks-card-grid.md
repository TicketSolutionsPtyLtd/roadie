Add `CardGrid` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/card-grid`), a block of like items, such as benefits, services, steps, contact options, or people, as cards in columns that follow the block's own width. Its presets are `cards`, `marked-cards`, `overlap-cards`, `portraits`, and `lists`. In `cards`, the content picks the card: an image with an illustration overlaps it, `numbered` numbers the cards, and an item with an `href` links its title and makes the whole card clickable. `itemEmphasis` sets every card's surface, and an item's `intent` tints its own card.
