A block's action `icon` that names no icon, such as `constructor`, draws no icon. An `illustration` that names no spot illustration draws none on a card or stat, and the block's default on Hero, RichText, and CallToAction. A block's or Mosaic tile's `size`, or a Gallery's or CallToAction's `ratio`, that names none of its values draws as the default.
