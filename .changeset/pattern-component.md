Add `Pattern` at `@oztix/roadie-components/pattern`, the Oztix swirl pattern. It fills its parent, and `crop` (`top`, `left`, `right`, or `banner`, default `right`) picks the swirl for where it sits. `tone` (`brand-blue`, `brand-purple`, or `brand-orange`, default `brand-blue`) picks its colour, which stays the same in light and dark mode. It's always hidden from assistive technology.
