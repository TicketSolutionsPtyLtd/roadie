Add a board layout to `Records`: `boardLayout({ groupBy, title, onMove })` from `@oztix/roadie-components/record-board` shows a column for each option of the `groupBy` field, in option order, then "No <field>", saved as `{ type: 'board', groupBy: [field] }`. Cards move to another column by drag, or from the Move menu on their handle. `onMove(row, changes)` gets the grouping fields the move changed, such as `{ status: 'sold_out' }`, with null for "No <field>", to save as a patch (`RecordGroupChanges`), and a rejection puts the card back with its message. On a phone the columns sit in a row that scrolls sideways, each about 85% of the width, so the next column peeks in. Give `groupBy` two fields, such as `groupBy: ['activity', 'milestone']`, and the second makes lanes: one per option in option order, then "No <field>", each shown even when empty, with a heading holding its title, count and the field's `groupHeading` content. A lane collapses from its title for the session, the column headers stay in view as the lanes scroll, and cards move between columns within their lane. A view's `groupBy` wins over the layout's, and a third level isn't drawn.
