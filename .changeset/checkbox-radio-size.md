Add `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) to `Checkbox`, `CheckboxGroup`, and `RadioGroup`, and `trailing` to `Checkbox` and `RadioGroup.Item` for a count or value at the end of the row. The default `md` box is now 20px, down from 24px, which also makes RecordTable's and Records' selection checkboxes smaller; pass `size='lg'` to keep the old size. A radio's dot is now half its circle at every size.
