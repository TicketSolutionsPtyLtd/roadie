Add `CTABand` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/cta-band`), `Gallery` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/gallery`), and `FAQ` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/faq`), blocks that close a page with its next step, show its people and events in portrait photos, and answer its questions in an accordion.

`CTABand` has one preset, `band`: with an `image`, the pitch sits beside a photo that breaks out of the panel, tilted unless `tilted` is `false`, with an `illustration` at its corner; without one, it's centred. Its `emphasis` adds `strong` and `inverted`, on which its copy and strong action stay readable. `Gallery` has `carousel`, photos to step through, and `marquee`, photos scrolling past that hold still under reduced motion. `FAQ` has `accordion`, with each answer set in `Prose`, and `itemEmphasis` for its questions (`subtler`, `subtle`, or `normal`).
