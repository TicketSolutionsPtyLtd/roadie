Add `CTABand` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/cta-band`), `Gallery` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/gallery`), and `FAQ` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/faq`), blocks that close a page with its next step, show its people and events in portrait photos, and answer its questions in an accordion.

`CTABand` has the `with-photo` preset, the pitch beside a tilted photo, and `centred`, the pitch centred. `Gallery` has `carousel`, photos to step through, and `marquee`, photos scrolling past that hold still under reduced motion. `FAQ` has `accordion`, with each answer set in `Prose` and optional actions under the questions.
