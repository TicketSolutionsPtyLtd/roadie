Add grouping to `tableLayout()` and `RecordTable`: a view's `layout.groupBy` groups the rows by up to three fields with options. A wide table heads each group with a row spanning its columns, holding the group's title, count and heading content, and the title's button collapses the group's rows. Narrow rows group as the list's do. The table's settings gain Group by, Then by and And then by, and changing the columns keeps the grouping. A grouped table renders every row on its page, and a table that loads by range stays ungrouped.
