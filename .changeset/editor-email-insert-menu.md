Add the insert menu to the `email` preset: type `/` to insert a section, two to four columns, an image, a spacer, a divider, or a text block, filtered as you type. The email's content sits in a 600px canvas, and columns stack below 480px, as they do on a phone. The canvas spaces its blocks as the email does: 16px between blocks, 16px above and below a section, and 24px between side-by-side columns, with headings at the email's sizes. A section or a column doesn't pad or border its blocks; it shows a dashed outline, outside its box, under the pointer or while the caret is in it.
