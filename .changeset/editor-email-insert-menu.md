Add the insert menu to the `email` preset: type `/` to insert a section, two to four columns, an image, a spacer, a divider, or a text block, filtered as you type. The email's content sits in a 600px canvas with its sections and columns outlined, and columns stack below 480px, as they do on a phone. An image uploaded into an email must be served over https; a path on the site shows an error instead.
