Add grouping to `gridLayout()` and `RecordGrid`: a view's `layout.groupBy` groups the cards by up to three fields with options, each group a titled section with its count and a grid of its cards, collapsible from its title, as the list's groups are. The grid's settings gain Group by, Then by and And then by, and changing the card's details keeps the grouping. A grouped grid renders every card on its page, and a grid that loads by range stays ungrouped.
