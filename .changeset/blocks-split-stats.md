Add `Split` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/split`), `Splits` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/splits`), and `Stats` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/stats`), blocks that set text beside media, list like items as rows beside images, and show key numbers on their own.

`Split` is one section, with three presets: `image-start` and `image-end`, the text after or before an `image`; and `stats`, long-form text beside key figures that wrap below on narrow screens. `Splits` has one, `marked`: rows of a marked title and text beside a square image, alternating sides. `Stats` has one, `figures`: a heading over a wrapping grid of figures, each as written, with a label and an optional spot illustration.
