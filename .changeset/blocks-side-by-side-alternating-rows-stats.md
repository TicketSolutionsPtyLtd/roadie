Add `SideBySide` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/side-by-side`), `AlternatingRows` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/alternating-rows`), and `Stats` (`@oztix/roadie-blocks/stats`), blocks that set text beside media, list like items as rows beside images, and show key numbers on their own.

`SideBySide` is one section, with three presets: `image-start` and `image-end`, the text after or before an `image` cropped to its `ratio` (`square` by default); and `stats`, long-form text beside key figures that wrap below on narrow screens. `AlternatingRows` has one, `text-blocks`: `rows` of a marked title and text beside a square image, alternating sides. `Stats` has one, `figures`: a heading over a wrapping grid of `stats`, each as written, with a label and an optional spot illustration.
