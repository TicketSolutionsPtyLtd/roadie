Add smart punctuation to every preset: straight quotes curl, `...` becomes an ellipsis and `--` an em dash as you type, and `Backspace` straight after puts back what was typed. `Backspace` at the start of a heading turns it into a paragraph, as Notion does. `Mod+Z` and `Mod+Shift+Z` in an input of a custom block's `reactNodeView` now undo and redo its edits through the editor's history, where before they did nothing.
