Add `CtaBand` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/cta-band`), `Media` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/media`), and `Faq` (`@oztix/roadie-marketing-blocks/faq`), blocks that close a page with its next step, show its people and events in a photo marquee, and answer its questions in an accordion.

`CtaBand` has the `studio-cta` preset, the pitch beside a tilted photo, and `talk-to-us`, the pitch centred. `Media` has `gallery`, portrait photos that hold still under reduced motion. `Faq` has `faq`, with each answer set in `Prose`.
