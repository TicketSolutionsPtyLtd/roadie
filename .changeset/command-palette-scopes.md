`CommandPalette.Scopes` adds type tabs that narrow the commands by their `scope`, each as wide as its label. Tab and Shift+Tab switch them from the search, as they switch operator in `QueryField`, and move focus on past the last type or before All; the left and right arrows switch them too. `CommandPalette.Footer` holds `CommandPalette.Hint`s that show its keys as small keycaps.
