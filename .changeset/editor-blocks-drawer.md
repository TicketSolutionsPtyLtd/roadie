The email editor has a Blocks drawer. Blocks, at the start of the docked toolbar and the end of the floating toolbar, opens a drawer of tiles from the right, or from the bottom on a phone. A tile adds its block after the block with the caret.

The email preset's `/` menu and the blocks preset's slash menu now list their blocks under headings: Text, Media, Layout and, in email, Email. An app's own block goes under More, or under the heading its new `group` names.
