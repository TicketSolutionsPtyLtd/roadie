The blocks and email presets say what to do on an empty line: "Type / to add a block", or "Type / for blocks, or pick a section" in an email, on an empty document and on the empty line with the caret while the editor has focus. A `placeholder` replaces it, and still shows only while the editor is empty. An empty email also shows Start your email under its first line, with Sections and Blocks, which open the Blocks drawer at its sections or its blocks, and Import. The skeleton keeps its room, so the email doesn't grow as it loads. A ready-made email section's placeholder copy, such as Headline, now shows as a hint on its empty lines rather than as text to delete, and isn't saved.
